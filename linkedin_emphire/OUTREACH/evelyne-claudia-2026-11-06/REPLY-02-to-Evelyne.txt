The sentence about indistinguishability was too broad and I withdraw it as written. Measured, the two cells differ at every surface a person reads. The reply differs. The journal differs. The host's own problem list differs too, one entry in the accepted cell against four in the refused one. What is identical is narrower than I said: the three host event kinds and the withholding reason, which is "store reply rejected" in both. What the refused reply fails to do is name which write was refused, and that is all I can defend.

On the second point, you are right and my table let one path stand for both. The wire cells carry a BLOCK at sequence 4 and an ALLOW at sequence 5 against the same descriptor, so the disagreement is the verdict value. The host cells carry two BLOCK records, decision times 4 and 2, with different descriptors, so the disagreement is the descriptor and the clock. The first of those two is the verdict the host already held, which the run names as the seeded record at sequence 4, and the second is the one the command under test wrote. Both pairs are two different verdicts for one action id, which is why the same rule text applies to both, and they are not the same difference.

The retry is scripted, as you say, and I will not put it more strongly than that.
