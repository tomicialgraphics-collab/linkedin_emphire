# Video 1, first post on camera

Length: 60 to 75 seconds. Vertical, 9:16. One take if you can.
Files: `demo_video01.py` in this folder, run it live on screen.

Rule for the whole video: do not read a script. Learn the five beats below and say them in your own words. The lines written here are what the beats mean, not what to recite.

---

## Spoken script

**Beat 1, hook. 0 to 4 seconds. Straight to camera, no intro, no "hi everyone".**

> This check passed. The second time, it had nothing to check.

**Beat 2, the demo. 4 to 25 seconds. Screen recording of the terminal.**

Run it and let the output sit.

> Four runs. Clean to start. Then a record inserted into a slot already taken, and the check catches it. Remove the record proving it, and it passes. Empty the history, and it passes again.

**Beat 3, the reason. 25 to 45 seconds. Back to camera.**

> Nothing is broken. The check reads the records it is handed. Hand it none, and there is nothing to disagree with, so it says clean.

> That is not a bug in the model or in the check. It is a missing word. "I could not look" and "I looked and it was clean" have to be two different answers. If your system has one word for both, it will always pick the comforting one.

**Beat 4, the turn. 45 to 60 seconds.**

> It is one of the most expensive failures in production AI, and no dashboard shows it, because a pass looks like a pass.

**Beat 5, close. 60 to 70 seconds. Question, then stop. Do not add a call to action.**

> So, one question. When your agent cannot reach the log it is supposed to check, what does it tell you.

## Delivery notes

- Look at the lens, not the screen.
- Speak about 10 percent slower than feels natural. First videos are always too fast.
- No music. No jump cuts. No "let me show you something".
- Do not say your name, do not say what you do, do not ask for a follow. The video is the introduction.
- Captions on, burned in. Most people watch muted, and the fourth card is the line you want them to read.
- Film it twice. Post the second one. The first take is always the rehearsal.

## Caption for the post

> The check that passed because it had nothing left to check.
>
> Four runs in the video. The tampering is caught while the evidence is present, and the same code returns clean once the record proving it is removed. Nothing in the checker is broken. The vocabulary is.
>
> "I could not look" and "I looked and it was clean" are different findings and they have to print differently. Most pipelines I read have one word for both, and it is the reassuring one.

## What this video is doing for you

It opens with a result and not a claim, it shows code running, and it ends on a question that a reviewer, an auditor or a hiring manager would recognise as their own problem. That is the whole positioning in one minute, and it does not mention any client, any protocol, or any review in progress.

It also sets up the series. Video 2 is the fix. Video 3 is what happens when the log itself is the thing that failed. Do not promise either of those out loud in this one.
