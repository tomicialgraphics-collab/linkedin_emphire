# Tolu, studied

What this is: the shape of Tolulope Michael's posts and cards, measured, so the emphire can borrow the form. Who it is for:
the owner, who chose these as the model for posts that read as human and convert. What to do next: answer the four
decisions at the end; nothing in the registry changes until then.

His posts are input, never copy. He is in the owner's network (the owner commented on his post on 12 November), so a
borrowed sentence would be noticed by the one person most likely to notice.

## What the posts do, by count

- **135 posts.** Median 185 words. The owner's recent posts run 250 to 400.
- **112 of 135 end on a "PS:" question.** The question is small and answerable: "Which of these 5 skills do you think leaders
  struggle with the most?"
- **91 use a list**, numbered or dashed, almost always three to five items, each with a two-word label and one line under it.
- **22 are first-person stories** with a scene: a son asking about Saturn, a job interview that went wrong, a hire made from
  a badly written post. These are the longest and, by the look of the set, the ones he invests in.
- **6 carry a link**, nearly all to his own videos, written as three dashed lines of "this video shows".
- **5 are faith posts**, short, on Sundays.

## Why they read as human

1. **Line one is a claim a reader can hold**, under twelve words: "Talent alone won't take you to the top."
2. **Line two turns it**, short: "It might look harsh but that's the truth."
3. **One idea per post.** Never two.
4. **Plain words, short lines, white space.** Every paragraph is one to three lines on a phone.
5. **The reader is "you".** His own story is used to hand the reader something, never to show a result.
6. **The ending asks for one small reply**, which is how the posts collect comments.

## What not to take

- **His unsourced figures.** "Awareness training can cut breaches by as much as 70%", "why 80% still fail", "pays up to $90k".
  None carries a source. R06 forbids this, and for a brand built on checking claims it would be the fastest way to lose it.
- **Stories that cannot be dated.** T2 already says only real events on real dates.
- **The generic subjects.** He writes for career changers. The owner sells to engineering leads and hiring panels, so the
  form transfers and the subject does not. Leadership and career posts would pull the wrong followers (the growth plan's
  own lane rule).

## The cards

Five layouts recur, all 4:5 (800x1000; the owner's 1080x1350 is the same shape, sharper):

| Layout | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| A. Photo with a post card | a real portrait, full bleed, with a white rounded card over it: small avatar, name, verified tick, role line, one sentence |
| B. Headline box | a bold headline in a black box top left, cut-out portrait below, a yellow "follow" tag |
| C. Dark quote | black ground, avatar and name, one or two sentences in white, nothing else |
| D. Diagram | light ground, bold title, a roadmap or a numbered list drawn as a picture |
| E. Quote with callouts | a sentence in a black box over a black-and-white portrait, two dotted callouts, a yellow "Repost" pill |

The palette is already the owner's: off-white, black, and a yellow close to `#F9D811`. The card rebuilt on 15 November is a
version of layout B.

**The binding constraint is photographs, not design.** The 34 cards on the contact sheet alone use more than ten different professional photos in different clothes and
rooms. The owner has one headshot, 400x400. Layouts A, B and E need real photographs, and R25 forbids a generated face.
A two-hour shoot giving eight to ten photos is what unlocks the set.

Two things not to copy on a card: a verified tick the owner does not have (the profile shows "Verify in 2 minutes", so
verify first), and his name, which reached two renders by mistake once already (`ASSETS/brand/README.md`).

## One post in his shape, on the owner's own material

Built on T2, from a real event already recorded in this repository (the 16-of-64 verifier finding). Not gated yet.

> I built a tool to catch broken checks.
>
> It had a broken check.
>
> My verifier compared 16 characters of every fingerprint instead of all 64. It passed fifty checks with zero failures.
> An independent reviewer found the hole in ten minutes.
>
> Here's what I do differently now:
>
> 1. Feed it something bad first. If a check has never failed, it hasn't been tested.
> 2. Hand it to someone else. The builder is the worst person to break their own tool.
> 3. Write down what it can't see. A limit you name is a limit nobody trips over.
>
> I don't trust a green result anymore until I've watched it go red.
>
> PS: When did you last try to break your own tool?

## Four decisions for the owner

1. **The PS question.** R15 warns on any post that ends on a question, and 112 of his 135 do. Allow a closing "PS:" question
   on posts for two weeks and measure comments, or keep R15 as it is?
2. **The video teaser shape.** Add it as T15 for article and playbook links, or not?
3. **The cards.** R25 locks card geometry. Lift it to add layouts A, C, D and E beside the current B?
4. **The photo shoot.** Book one, so the cards can carry real photographs?

The registry, the templates and the card renderer stay as they are until these four are answered.
