## ICP - who actually buys AI red teaming (verified market signals)

Segment A - mid-market AI deployers (50-1,000 staff). Signal:
IANS/Artico benchmark, published 2026-10-22: 65% of $10B+ revenue
orgs have assigned a head of AI security, but the role is DEDICATED
at only 9% of $1B-$10B firms and 6% of under-$1B firms, where it
falls to GRC/SecOps/architecture leads. Translation: the
responsibility exists, the team does not. They outsource red teaming
and evals. Buyer: CISO / VP Security / AI program lead.

Segment B - AI-native product companies (10-200 staff) selling to
enterprise or raising. Trigger: enterprise security questionnaires,
investor DD, public agent launches. Buyer: CTO / founder / head of
security. Corroboration: AgenticCareers (Sep 2026) tracks 45 open AI
security roles across 24 companies, median advertised $192K-$270K,
most active employers Vercel, Harvey, Brex, Twilio, Ramp. SANS
tracker (Oct 2026): 850+ open AI/ML security jobs, ~100 of them
red-team specialist, median $124K. Hiring is the budget proof, and
hiring is slow, which is exactly the gap interim red-team capacity
fills.

Segment C - regulated enterprises deploying AI agents (fintech,
banking, healthcare, government-adjacent). Triggers: EU AI Act
(Digital Omnibus adopted 2026-07-27: Article 50 transparency live
2026-08-02; machine-readable marking for legacy generative systems
due 2026-12-02, ~5 weeks away; high-risk Annex III deferred to
2027-12-02; penalties to EUR 15M/3%), board AI-risk mandates, agent
incidents. Buyer: CISO / risk lead.
