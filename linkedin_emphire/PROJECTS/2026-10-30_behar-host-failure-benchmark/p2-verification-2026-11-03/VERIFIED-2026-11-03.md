## The lesson worth keeping

Both failures were mechanical, both were caught by a checksum, and neither was a content problem. A pathspec
that looks like a shell glob, and a line ending rule that looks like a formatting preference, were enough to
make a repository fail two manifests it had previously satisfied. That is the argument of the whole review,
arriving in the tidy-up rather than in the code.
