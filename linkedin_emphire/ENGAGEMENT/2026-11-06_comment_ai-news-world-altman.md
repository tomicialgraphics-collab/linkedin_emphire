named map accounts or niche-verbatim posts. This is neither. It is an aggregator republishing a

## Draft, paste as a comment, 128 words

Both questions collapse into one, and neither is answerable yet.

How smart AI should be next to humans needs a definition of smart you can compute. At the moment that
definition is whatever the benchmark author picked, and it changes with the version. So the argument
is happening on top of a layer that keeps moving.

Speed has the same problem from the other side. Improvement is partly set by how fast evaluation keeps
up, and evaluation is the slower half. Capability lands before anyone can say what it bought.

The power question is not separate either. Too few hands is a claim about who gets to measure and
certify, and that is nobody's instrumented number.

You cannot set a dial on a reading you do not have.

## Why this shape
