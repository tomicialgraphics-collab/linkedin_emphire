## The correction on CFO and PM

The request named CEO, CTO, CFO and PM. Two of those do not buy this.

**CFO.** A $150 scan, a $600 audit or a $2,500 engagement is signed by a founder or a CTO, from a budget that already exists. A CFO enters at board-report level or above roughly $50M revenue, which is not the tier being sold to. Time spent on CFOs is time not spent on the person who says yes in one meeting.

**PM.** Product managers cannot authorise spend at this size, and they are the wrong reader for a security report. They are, however, excellent for reach: they share, they comment, and they sit inside the product teams that will eventually need the check. Treat them as audience, not pipeline.

**The titles that actually convert**, in order: Founder, Co-founder, CTO, VP Engineering, Head of AI, Head of Security, CISO. The ICP work of 25 October reached the same conclusion from the other direction: the buyer is the person whose deal dies in a security questionnaire, and that is a founder or a CTO, not a finance function.

## The engine, so the list is not a one-off

LinkedIn's own search, which cannot be reached from here, returns fresher and better-ranked people than any web search can. Run these weekly, with the **second degree** filter on and the region set to United States and United Kingdom first, India and Nigeria second.

- `("co-founder" OR CTO) ("AI agents" OR agentic) security` in People
- `"Head of AI Security" OR "AI Security Lead"` in People
- `CISO ("AI governance" OR "agentic AI")` in People
- `founder (evals OR "AI evaluation" OR "LLM security")` in People
- Company-size filter 11 to 200, then read the last three posts before writing anything

Twenty a week, not two hundred. At 1,244 followers a mass connect reads as a bot and the accept rate collapses, which is the metric that matters here.

## The note, and the guardrail

LinkedIn allows 300 characters on a connection request. Three shapes, no pitches, no links:

**Founder or CTO shipping agents.** "Following your work on [the specific thing]. I break AI agents for a living and publish the tests, so this is a peer connection rather than a pitch."

**Peer vendor.** "Same problem from the other side: I test what your platform claims. Happy to compare notes on [specific]."

**Higher-reach practitioner.** Comment twice first, then: "Enjoying the [topic] posts, particularly [one detail]. Connecting to keep reading."

The guardrail is this week's own analytics. 11,144 impressions produced nine attributed profile views and zero sends, and the account grew 560 followers in three weeks mostly from the profile and comment threads. Connections are not the bottleneck and were never going to be. What converts is a DM that arrives with a premise, of the kind already sent to Kuldeep, Marvin and Travis. The list exists to supply the names for those messages, not to inflate a count.
