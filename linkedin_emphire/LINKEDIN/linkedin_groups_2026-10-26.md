GROUP RULES OF ENGAGEMENT (all 20, from S16 + the no-slop rules):
1. NO hard sell, ever, in a group. No "DM me for a pentest."
   The pitch lives in the profile and in the DM after value.
2. Check each group's admin rules on day one: some ban
   external links (the five-tests repo CTA may not fit - if a
   group bans links, the artifact goes in the profile, and the
   group answer stands on its own).
3. Answer > post. In expert groups, a substantive answer to a
   member's question outperforms a cross-post. Cross-posting
   all 3 weekly posts into groups = spam pattern, demotes the
   account.
4. Buyer vocabulary per cluster (S3): T1 groups = "the agent",
   "the evals", "the questionnaire", "proof", "SOC 2";
   governance groups = "Article 50", "marking", "grace
   period", "audit trail", "the board"; finance groups =
   "board assurance", "who signed off on this".
5. The S19 gate still applies to group-sourced DMs: the two
   diagnostic questions BEFORE any price. No deal / launch /
   DD / questionnaire / dated trigger = the 97%, they get the
   content path, politely.

## CADENCE + CAP FIT (this is the part that keeps the account
## healthy):
- Groups count against the daily action cap (a group comment
  is a comment). Do NOT stack group activity on top of the
  comment queue.
- PRIMARY GROUPS: pick 3 to be active in, one per buyer
  cluster: #1 (Agentic AI Engineering) + #15 (RAI) + #19
  (Finance & Banking Fintech Regtech). 1-2 substantive
  answers each per week, on questions that exist - no
  self-starting threads until the group rules are known.
- SECONDARY: the other 17 get a weekly 5-minute scan: answer
  one good question if it exists, connect 1-3 active
  members who fit the ICP (their connect notes follow the
  Lantern 2 structure: specific observation + one line on
  what I do + shared artifact + 15-minute ask, no pitch in
  message one).
- POSTING INTO GROUPS: only where admin rules allow, and
  never the same post twice in one week across groups. The
  3 weekly posts go to the OWN feed first; a group post is a
  deliberate choice (usually the Article 50 mechanics post
  into #15/#18, an agent-incident teardown into #1/#3).
- THE DM CONVERSION (Lantern 3): group answer -> they reply ->
  value DM (a teardown or a specific observation) -> the
  five-tests repo ("run it, tell me what breaks") -> the
  30-minute diagnosis with the two S19 questions. No step is
  a hard sell.
- KILL CRITERIA (per the 30-day plan): after 30 days, any
  primary group that produced 0 ICP conversations gets
  swapped for a secondary. Groups are a means to the two
  diagnostic questions, not a hobby.
