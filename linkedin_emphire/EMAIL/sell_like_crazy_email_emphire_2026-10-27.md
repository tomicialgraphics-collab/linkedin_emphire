## 1. What the books say (only the parts that matter)

### Sell Like Crazy (Suby), the email chapter
E-1 EMAIL IS THE ONLY ASSET YOU OWN. "Email outperforms every other
marketing channel. Period." DMA: $44 back per $1 spent. McKinsey:
forty-fold impact. The risk: "It's a huge risk to build a business
on a platform owned by somebody else." LinkedIn can change policy
overnight. The list cannot be taken away. (SLC ~5117)
E-2 ONLY THREE THINGS MATTER: get it delivered, get it opened, get
it clicked. Everything else is decoration. (SLC ~5167)
E-3 DELIVERED = sender score + platform + plain text. Sender score
above 90 on senderscore.org, checked often. Warm up the new IP.
Plain text beats HTML (HubSpot data in the book: HTML decreased
opens and clicks). No logo in the header, no heavy images, no
HTML "masterpiece". A personal sender name, never a sales@
address. (SLC ~5215 to 5370)
E-4 OPENED = THE P GROUP. Readers sort the inbox into P (personal,
always opened) and C (commercial, deleted without opening). The
sender name, subject line, and preheader decide which group.
Getting into the P group is the entire game. (SLC ~5300 to 5360)
E-5 SUBJECT LINE: 1 to 20 characters (short and personal) or very
long. Never the 41 to 50 middle ("the death zone"). Lowercase or
sentence case, no all caps. A question, or enough to open but not
the whole story. Avoid formulaic subject lines ("7 secrets to
X"): they work once and then cause email blindness. (SLC ~5470 to
5560)
E-6 PREHEADER: the "sniff test". A mid-sentence hook, a
cliffhanger, never the whole story. (SLC ~5590 to 5670)
E-7 TIMING: Tuesday at 10 am is the number one slot in the data,
then Thursday 8 pm, then Wednesday 2 pm. Then: test against your
own list, because everyone is wrong until proven otherwise.
(SLC ~5670 to 5710)
E-8 READ AND CLICK = TONE THAT ENTERTAINS. "My number one fear when
I send an email is that it will be boring." Write like you talk to
your best friend. Short lines, small paragraphs. Specific and
visceral copy (vague copy is a one-way ticket to email blindness).
One CTA, and it is a command, not a question: "don't ask them to
buy, click or act, tell them". (SLC ~5715 to 5960)
E-9 GOODWILL = 2/3 CONTENT, 1/3 OFFER. "Make sure two thirds of
your emails are content and value, and one third is offers and
promos. Throw them in the P.S. and ensure the main body of the
email stands alone as a valuable piece of content." (SLC ~5265)
E-10 THE 97% FORMULA: the 3% are in buying mode and take the
offer immediately. The 97% are the real market, nurtured by a
value sequence that moves them one step closer to their outcome at
every step. Each piece: 80% value, 20% pitch at the end. (SLC
~4355 to 4480)
E-11 HINT AT WHAT'S COMING. "You always want to hint in your emails
that there's something else coming... This makes them eager to
open up all your emails and see what treasure is inside."
(SLC ~4565)
E-12 THE SWIPE FILE. Sign up for every newsletter in your industry.
Study what they send, how often, what subjects, what CTAs. Then do
the opposite. "Study the herd and do the opposite." (SLC ~5575,
~5885)
E-13 EMAIL BLINDNESS IS THE DISEASE. Boring emails make you
invisible in the inbox. The only cure: write emails that
entertain, excite, and engage. (SLC ~5395)

## 3. The weekly verified research loop

One slot per week. One number. Produced the week before the
Tuesday send. Owner budget: 2 to 3 hours.

THE CANDIDATE POOL (fed continuously, no separate research time):
1. The LINKEDIN feed captures (named claims with numbers)
2. The comment performance log (numbers that already performed)
3. Public security events (CVEs, patches, disclosed exploits)
4. The EU AI Act dated milestones (2 Dec marking grace, 2027
   Annex III)
5. Vendor benchmarks from the pipeline conversations (foreign
   named claims preferred, per the foreign-first pivot)

THE QUALITY GATE (a candidate becomes the weekly research only
if all six pass):
1. It is a named claim with a number, not a vibe
2. It is re-runnable (public source, or reproducible with the
   five-tests repo)
3. The re-run was actually run by the owner, with the
   fingerprint (model, seed, temperature, prompt version, date)
   "verified" means we ran it, not that we cited it
4. It is foreign-first, or the Nigeria trust lane as secondary
5. No third-party consent gate (public information, or the
   owner's own published work)
6. It yields a real honest line (what it does not prove)

PRODUCTION STEPS:
1. Pick the candidate, log it in the RESEARCH LOG
2. Run the re-run, record the fingerprint
3. Write the four lines (claim, result, method, honest line)
4. Write the two framings: the practitioner first paragraph and
   the decision-maker first paragraph (the personalization
   engine, section 5)
5. Subject: 1 to 20 characters, lowercase, personal
6. Teaser: next week's number, only if it is real
7. Log the send state (DRAFTED until the owner says SENT, R10)

THE HONEST WEEK: if no candidate passes the gate, send the short
honest email ("no number broke this week. that is a good thing.
the last one was X, and here is the state of it."). Cadence is
kept, nothing is fabricated, and the honesty is a P-group
signal. (No-fabrication rule, E-8, R8.)

## 4. The 500 verified email plan

DEFINITION: verified = double opt-in confirmed (the person
clicked the confirmation link) AND the consent record exists
(protocol R1). 500 is the list maturity target and the point at
which the list moves to owned infrastructure (listmonk on a $5
VPS; the move decision is made at ~300, the migration lands by
~500, protocol platform section). It is not the revenue gate: the
stage 2 offer gate fires at 100.

LANE 0, THE SEED (Hormozi L3, "everyone has a list"):
The existing clients (the 5+, including the Upwork client files)
plus the 30-name warm list. Personal consent DMs, A-C-A, max
three touches (L4, L5). Copy:
  "Quick ask. I'm sending one short note a week when I re-run an
  AI number and it breaks. The re-run is in the note. Your
  address, one click to leave. Want it?"
Expected take: 50 percent of the warm asks (a warm ask with a
real reason). First 15 to 20 verified. This lane runs in week 1,
in parallel with platform setup.

LANE 1, THE BUYER PIPELINE (the current campaign):
DM replies and warm comment replies, capture copy in
magnet_verification_receipt.md (three variants). Benchmarks from
the channel research in the $5K playbook: 15 to 25 percent of
touches reply; of the replies, about 40 percent give an address
when the reason is the receipt. At the current touch volume
(5 meaningful comments a day, 3 to 5 connection notes a day),
base velocity is about 15 to 30 verified a week.

LANE 2, THE CONTENT LANE:
The Article 50 CTA plus the one-pager (the 1 hour build, copy in
the magnet file). Article impressions are currently about 120 a
week, so the article CTA alone is 2 to 4 a week. The one-pager
is the multiplier: every vulnzero-format post (the 4x
out-performer in the 10-25 analytics) ends with the opt-in line
once every two weeks (the existing group CTA rotation (c),
re-pointed at the email).

LANE 3, REFERRALS (Hormozi L20):
After every personal receipt delivery, one line:
  "If someone you know runs an agent like this and wants their
  own receipt, two slots a month, forward this line."
Receipt recipients are the warmest referrers on the list, and
each one is a verified buyer-shaped contact who already saw the
full version's shape.

THE MATH (honest, logged for the tracker):
- Base case (current touch volume, no one-pager yet): 25 to 35
  verified a week, so 500 in about 5 to 7 months.
- Accelerated case (one-pager live, referral line live, the
  stage 2 closes start producing case-study readers): 60 to 100
  a week, so 500 in about 2 to 3 months.
- The revenue gate does not wait for 500: 100 is reached in the
  base case in about 3 to 4 weeks, which opens the stage 2 offer
  (the Agent Gate emails, HELD in sequence_02_offer.md).
- If the base velocity holds below 15 a week for three weeks:
  the magnet is the problem before the message is (L-13). Change
  the magnet wording or the capture reason, not the touch volume.

## 5. The personalization engine

"Personalized weekly" means three layers, run honestly at 500
scale.

THE CONTACT RECORD (one row per contact, the CONTACTS LOG plus
these fields):
- name: first name
- source: which DM, article, or comment, with a 5-word context
  line ("MCP thread, 10-27", "Article 50, EU deadline")
- room: practitioner or decision-maker (the two-room register)
- context: the topic they engaged on (MCP, EU AI Act, PHP,
  finance, voice, agents at banks)
- trigger: the dated trigger if known (questionnaire date, the
  2 December grace, a deal, a launch)
- receipt: none, requested, or delivered, with date
- reply state: never replied, replied date, 1:1 lane
- open state: last open date (for hygiene, below)

LAYER 1, THE FIRST LINE (merge, every email, every contact):
  "{first name}, {their context}."
Example: "Reed, you flagged the MCP thread last week." This is
L-12 (1 to 3 real facts) doing the P-group work (E-4). If a
contact has no context on file, the first line falls back to the
source line only, never to nothing.

LAYER 2, THE FRAMING (segment, two bodies per weekly research):
the same research, two first paragraphs.
- PRACTITIONER frame: method first. The result and the re-run
  lead, the consequence is one line at the end.
- DECISION-MAKER frame: consequence first. The forcing function
  leads (board, insurer, questionnaire, diligence, the 2
  December grace), the method is the one-sentence answer to
  "how would you know?", the close is the date or the decision.
The rest of the email (the four lines, the honest line, the
teaser) is shared.

LAYER 3, THE 1:1 LANE (hand, never batch):
every person who replies gets a same-day personal reply,
hand-written, not from a template. This is the 3% lane (E-10)
and the engaged-lead lane (L-1). It is the only lane that ever
carries the offer, and only after the gate (R2). Volume
expectation at 500: 30 to 80 people ever enter the 1:1 lane.
That is 30 to 80 hand-written notes, which is the actual sales
work of the division.

THE HONEST CAPACITY STATEMENT: at 500, layers 1 and 2 are
templated personalization (the first line and the framing come
from the record; two body versions per research). Layer 3 is
hand-written for the repliers. This is how small brands run
"personal" at scale. (The Obama comparison, E-5: 20 writers is
not the model. The P-group mechanics are.)

HYGIENE: three consecutive non-opens, the contact moves to the
quiet list (monthly sends, same content). Sender reputation is
driven by engagement (E-3), and the quiet list is how a 500 list
keeps a 95 sender score.

  E4 "run this on your own agent" (26) fix, "run this"

  "when it's yours"
  E6 "one question" (12) pass
  W "one number, re-run (date)" (25) fix, "one number"
PREHEADER: the first visible line, written as a mid-sentence
hook. It is already the body's first line in sequence 01.
TIMING: the data (E-7) says Tuesday 10 am, recipient local
time. The list is foreign-first and mixed US/EU, so the default
is Tuesday 10 am Eastern (9 am in Lagos, 3 pm in London in
winter, 10 am in New York), reviewed quarterly against the open
and reply data, switched to European morning if the list
composition turns EU-heavy.
CADENCE: one personalized research email a week (the W spine).
New arrivals run E1 to E6 over 28 days. If an onboarding email
lands on a W week for that contact, the W wins and the
onboarding email slides one week. One send a contact a week,
never two, except a 1:1 lane reply (which is a reply, not a
send).
THE P.S. SLOT: stage 1, the P.S. carries the receipt line (two a
month, free), which is the magnet, not a sale, so it is allowed
under E-9. Stage 2, the P.S. carries the gate line.
