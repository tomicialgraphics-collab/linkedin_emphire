# Visual options: value pluralism post

For the version the owner is holding, which is the text now in `post.md`: headline "Your red team
has values. It just hasn't written them down."

306 words, gate 100/100. Rules come from `DAILY CONTENT/visual_and_hook_spec.md`.

## The first line, judged

"Your red team has values. It just hasn't written them down." is 59 characters, so it survives
every cut point. Two clauses, and the second one turns the first, which is the mechanism that
works. You cannot finish the thought from the first half alone, which is the whole test.

**Ship it. Do not add a hook line above it.** A post that stacks a hook on a headline has two
opening lines and neither one is the opening line.

**The one weakness.** Nothing above the fold is a number. Headline plus subheadline is 181
characters, which fits, but the pair makes a claim and offers no evidence, so the reader has to take
"has values" on trust. One line fixes it, and it is in the card options below.

## Card 1: recommended

Keeps the owner's sentence. Boxes the turn, which is the only part of the headline that describes a
failure rather than a subject.

```
HEADLINE   YOUR RED TEAM
           HAS VALUES. IT JUST
           [HASN'T WRITTEN THEM DOWN]

SUB        ANTHROPIC BUILT ONE CONSTITUTION FROM 275 PUBLIC STATEMENTS.
           BOTH SIDES OF A REAL CONFLICT WERE DROPPED.
```

**Box:** HASN'T WRITTEN THEM DOWN. Four words, which breaks the one-to-three rule on purpose. The
clause dies when it is cut. "NOT WRITTEN" loses who failed to write it, and "UNWRITTEN" turns a
failure into a mood.

**Why the sub changes.** "Value pluralism says there is no single rubric" is a definition. A card is
read in two seconds and a definition gives the reader nothing to hold. The 275 is the reason to
believe the headline, and it is true (see `sources.md`).

**If the sub changes, change it in the post too.** Card and post carry the same pair, or the design
and the text disagree in public. Swap in the post's second line:

> Anthropic built one constitution from 275 public statements. Both sides of a real conflict were
> dropped.

Word count stays 306 and the gate stays clean. Body copy still carries the 50% overlap and the two
excluded statements, so the figure is reinforced rather than repeated.

## Card 2: mechanism led

Moves the thesis out of paragraph four and into the headline. Strongest of the three for a buyer who
reads fast.

```
HEADLINE   YOUR RED TEAM ISN'T
           TESTING THE MODEL.
           IT'S [MEASURING ITSELF]

SUB        275 PUBLIC STATEMENTS WENT INTO ONE CONSTITUTION.
           BOTH SIDES OF A REAL CONFLICT WERE DROPPED.
```

**Box:** MEASURING ITSELF. Two words, a verb, and it passes the portability test because no other
post could use it.

**Cost:** it drops the phrase "has values", which is the pluralism hook. The reader gets the failure
before they get the subject.

## Card 3: number led

For a research audience rather than a buyer. Anthropic Fellows, Aithos, alignment people.

```
HEADLINE   275 STATEMENTS.
           BOTH SIDES OF ONE
           CONFLICT [DROPPED]

SUB        THE MODEL THAT CAME OUT WITHDREW ON CONTESTED QUESTIONS.
           A SINGLE RUBRIC WOULD CALL THAT SAFER.
```

**Box:** DROPPED. One word, a failure verb, and it carries the whole card.

**Cost:** "a single rubric would call that safer" is an argument, not a finding. It follows from the
post but nobody measured it, so it belongs on a card only if the owner is comfortable arguing it in
the comments. That sentence is framed as a claim about the rubric, not about the model, which is why
it is defensible.
