# First comment

The test I keep coming back to is crude. Take one refusal, throw the reply away, and try to reconstruct it from the records alone: which rule fired, at what version, what the caller was allowed to see.

If that reconstruction needs the person who wrote the guard, the trace is not a record. It's a memory.

Same criterion applies to failures, and a failure is easier to prove because something breaks visibly. A refusal is quieter. Nothing fails, the caller gets told no, and the day goes fine until someone asks why.
