The monitor experiments are the most useful part and the hardest to keep durable. A synchronous monitor is a component that fails open, and a benchmark has no way of knowing when it has, so an evasion that works today quietly becomes a score tomorrow. There's a cheap fix borrowed from security teams testing their own detections: a handful of canary items whose answer exists only somewhere the model should never reach, scored before any real task, with any hit read as a monitor failure rather than a model win. That separates a working monitor from an absent one, which from the outside look exactly alike.
