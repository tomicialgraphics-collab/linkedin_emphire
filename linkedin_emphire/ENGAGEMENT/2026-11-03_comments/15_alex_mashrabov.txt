The line worth keeping is the one about treating every critique as a list of things to fix. What separated that team from everyone calling CG a toy had less to do with the twenty years and more to do with keeping an honest list and working it down without flinching. AI video has the same option now. Everybody can see what's wrong with a clip, and almost nobody writes it down next to the prompt and the seed that produced it, so the next release gets judged on how it feels rather than on whether the defect actually closed. Pixar reached Toy Story because the list was real.
