# ICP-50: founders with a claim to prove and a reason to prove it now

What this is: 50 companies matching `STRATEGY/2026-11-20_icp-and-selling-point_verified.md`, each with a named founder, a
dated trigger and the source. Who it is for: the owner, to send by hand. What to do next: work tier A from the top, two to
three a day, using the opener at the end, and log each send in `STATE/REVENUE-LOG.md` the day it becomes a conversation.

## What is verified, and what is not

- **Verified:** the company, the founder's name and role, the trigger and its date, against the linked source.
- **Not verified:** email addresses and LinkedIn profile URLs. None is given, because a guessed address is a fabricated
  one (R06). Find each founder by name and company on LinkedIn at the moment of sending.
- **Single-source names** are marked. Check the company's own site before using them.
- Amounts and customer names are what each company announced, not checked independently.

## The tiers

- **A, 28 rows:** sellers of AI agent security, guardrails, identity or governance. Their product is a control claim, and
  enterprise buyers will ask who tested it. Closest to the seven who already answered.
- **B, 18 rows:** AI agents sold into banks, insurers, hospitals, law and audit firms. Their buyers run vendor reviews.
- **C, 4 rows:** strong fit, older trigger (2025). Work last.

Klaimee (B) is a partner lead as well as a buyer: it certifies AI agents before insuring them, so independent testing
is part of its own process.

Left out on purpose: companies that sell AI red teaming or agent testing themselves (General Analysis, Plurai, Klarent),
because they are competitors, and companies past 100 staff or Series B (Onyx Security).

## The opener, diagnosis before price

Sell Like Crazy, Phase 7: no prescription without a diagnosis. Two questions first, no price in the first message.

> Congratulations on the round. Quick question from someone who tests agent controls for a living: when a customer's
> security team asks who independently tested [the claim their product makes], what do you hand them today?

If they answer with a named customer or a date, the second message asks: who is asking, and by when? A named asker and
a date is a budget. No asker and no date goes on a follow-up date in the revenue log, not into a proposal.

Send by hand, two or three a day, on LinkedIn. Nothing is sent by the agent (R28).
