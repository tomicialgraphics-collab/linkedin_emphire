# Coverage argument

Generated by `python3 tools/coverage.py status.json`. This file is the shape it takes, with the notes a reader needs around it.

## How to read this

Four buckets, and the count in each. The one that matters most is `open`, because a coverage argument that does not state it is an advertisement.

- **Tested.** A case exists, with a revision and a date.
- **Absent.** The target does not have this class. Confirmed, not assumed.
- **Skipped.** Deliberately not tested. The reason is mandatory.
- **Open.** Not tested. No apology, only a number.

## What the numbers mean for this engagement

Two or three sentences. Which categories were the focus, which were out of scope by agreement, and what the shape of the gaps says about confidence in the headline findings.

## Classes tested but with a weak result

If a rate came from fewer than twenty attempts, or from a single run, say so here rather than in a table where it will be read as solid.

## What would change the conclusion

The two or three tests that would most likely move the overall judgement, and why they were not run.
