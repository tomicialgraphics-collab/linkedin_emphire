## Not claimed

- No claim about code quality, defect rates or revert rates in either group. GitHub does not publish them, and nobody else has a credible figure yet.
- No claim that merge rate rises because reviewers are tired. It is offered as an alternative explanation, not an assertion.
- No claim about any vendor, tool or team.
- No mention of the benchmark or the external review. The post stands on GitHub's numbers and the counting problem alone, so it does not depend on work still under review.
