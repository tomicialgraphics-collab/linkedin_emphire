# Marvin, on the phone, tonight

He said "call me", so call rather than send a link. It is 00:35 here and 18:35 his time. He has sent notes, which means he wants to walk them.

He is 74, he speaks slowly and expects you to keep up, and his notes are headings. Follow his headings. Thirty minutes, forty at the outside.

## 0:00 to 2:00. Open

"Marvin, I've got your notes in front of me. I'd rather go in your order and let you talk, and I'll ask where I disagree. And I've got answers to your four questions, so we don't run out of time on those."

Then let him start. Do not summarise his own notes back to him.

## 2:00 to 8:00. His pages, briefly

He will re-read the six points. Agree once, in one sentence, and add nothing: "All six are in, including not storing her number."

The only thing worth adding is the fifth state you raised and he has not yet answered: "When a witness withdraws, that's an event, not an edit. The certificate has to carry it, or the record can hide one."

## 8:00 to 16:00. Your four answers, ready to say

**What worries you.** Say this, it is the honest one:

"Two things. My worry isn't that it won't work, it's that it works and nobody pays. The buyer has no money at the moment of a sale, the agency has money and is buying speed, not proof. So the certificate can be perfect and still be a cost with no revenue behind it. The second worry is worse. A certificate that looks official will be used by somebody to sell land that shouldn't be sold, and when that happens the first question will be who issued it. I'd rather we design the wording for that day now than after it."

**How much time.** Give a number, because he will plan around whatever you say:

"Fifteen hours a week, most of it early morning my time before the paid work. Evenings are unreliable and Sundays are out. If it needs more than fifteen in a week, I'll tell you on the Monday rather than on the Friday."

**A surveyor who lost an argument about a version of a plan.** If you know one, name him. If not, say this:

"I don't have one yet. The honest way to find him is through a residents' association or a family that has already been through a dispute, not through LinkedIn. Give me a week and I'll bring you a name rather than a hope."

**The first property lawyer.** Same shape. Do not invent a name. Say who you would ask and by when.

## 16:00 to 24:00. What he is asking you to agree

He wants: start Monday 23 November, a three-line daily note, problems raised the same day, a Friday call at 3 p.m. his time, and Claude Pro paid.

Agree to the habits, because they cost nothing and they are how he will judge you. Then say the one condition:

"Yes to Monday the 23rd, with one condition. Send me cycle one as eleven named requirements before Monday, in writing. I'll build those eleven and nothing else, and the other thirty-four stay a list we re-order after we've seen eleven work. If I start on forty-five, you'll get forty-five half-done, and you'll know it by week two."

Then the week-four sentence, once:

"One more thing, said out loud because it's better now than in December. Four weeks of building for a Claude Pro subscription isn't a fee and I'm not pretending it is. I'll do the four weeks because I want to see how you review. What I want is for week four to end in a decision: what the next four cost and who pays, or a clean stop. I'm not asking for a number tonight. I'm asking that it exists by then."

If he agrees, "then it's settled", and move on. Do not negotiate it further tonight.

## 24:00 to 30:00. The name and the close

On "Eri Trail": "It types easily, which fixes my objection, and it keeps the word that means the ancestor. I'd keep it as a working name and ask five people, not two, before we print it on anything. I'm not the right person to bless an Igbo word and I'd rather say that than nod."

Close: "Send the documents tonight or tomorrow and I'll read them before Monday. I'll send you the three lines every evening from Monday. And I'll bring you one surveyor and one lawyer by Friday, or tell you why not."

## The four things to leave with

1. Cycle one named as eleven, in writing, before Monday.
2. Week four ends in a decision about money, agreed out loud.
3. The Friday call time confirmed in his time and yours.
4. One name, or a date by which you'll produce one.

## Do not

Do not agree to forty-five in four weeks. Do not name a fee, a split or an equity number. Do not promise a surveyor you don't have. Do not let the name conversation run past three minutes. Do not let him talk past forty minutes without saying "Marvin, I need to stop at the hour".
