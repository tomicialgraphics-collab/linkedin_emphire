## What not to say

No mention of the money again, no hint that the acknowledgement is a substitute for the fee, and no thanks beyond the one
opening line. No promise to contribute code, no offer to join the Observatory, and nothing that accepts the framing of a
collaboration, which would make the review an inside job. No claim that the defects are confirmed by him, since his review
is still running.

## Why this is worth sending

The distinction is the whole asset. A review that becomes a contribution can no longer be cited as an outside check, and
his own contributors page already has the category that keeps it honest. Agreeing the sequence before writing protects
both of us from a piece that has to be retracted, and it costs one message.
