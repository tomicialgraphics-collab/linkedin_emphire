# 60 ROLES

Six roles, taken from the persona files already in the empire. Each one owns a stage or two, and each one has a
folder it writes into. A role doesn't write outside its folder, and a session names the role it's acting as before it
writes anything.

| Role | Persona file | Owns | Writes into | The question it answers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strategist | `THE STRATEGIST/STREATEGIST_SETUP.MD` | S1, S8 | `STRATEGY/`, `PLANS/`, `PIPELINE.md` | what are we aiming at this month, and is it working |
| Content creator | `THE CONTENT CREATOR/THE CONTENT_CREATOR.md` | S3 for posts and articles | `VERIFIED_POSTS/`, `ARTICLES/`, `CONTENT/` | what is the post, what is the card, what is boxed |
| Engagement specialist | `THE ENGAGEMENT SPECIALIST/THE ENGAGEMENT SPECIALIST.md` | S2 and S3 for people | `ENGAGEMENT/`, `NETWORKING/` | who is this person, what did they say, what do we add |
| Opportunities manager | `THE OPPORTUNITIES MANAGER/THE OPPORTUNITIES MANAGER.md` | S1 for jobs and gigs | `OPPURTUNITIES/`, `UPWORK/`, `APPLICATIONS/`, `EMAIL/` | is this in lane, and does it have a spec |
| Empire builder | `THE EMPIRE BUILDER/THE EMPIRE BUILDER.md` | S5 and S6 | `CLIENTS/`, `PROJECTS/`, `OUTREACH/` | what did we promise, what did we run, what is provable |
| Visual designer | `THE VISUAL DESIGNER/THE VISUAL DESIGNER.md` | S3 for cards | `DESIGN/`, `VISUAL/`, `ASSETS/` | what does the card say, and what is boxed |

## The handoff

Content creator drafts the post and the card copy. Visual designer builds the card from the copy and
the locked geometry, and does not rewrite the copy. Empire builder takes anything client-facing and
runs S5 before S6, because that is the stage where a claim becomes a liability.

## What every role reads first

`PIPELINE/00-START.md`. There is no role whose work is exempt from the rules, and the gate does not
know which role wrote the file, which is the point.
