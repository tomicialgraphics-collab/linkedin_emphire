## Why this is the whole of it

"I will keep you in mind" is the warmest thing a stranger says and the least actionable. What he keeps is a memory, and memories are made of sentences short enough to repeat. So the reply hands him the sentence, priced in three numbers and one promise about the report's honesty, which is the detail an auditor would notice.

The reciprocity is stated in one line and not negotiated. He is not being asked for a meeting, a favour, or an introduction, which is what keeps the door open rather than closing it politely.

The free second read is the only loose end, and it is deliberate: it gives him a reason to come back with an actual case rather than a polite memory.
