The honest answer from small teams is that governance doesn't get prioritised until something breaks, unless a policy is a thing the system can fail. What changes that is turning each policy into a test that runs without anyone remembering to run it. A row-level access rule becomes a query that attempts the read it should refuse, and retention becomes a job that reports what it deleted. Custodians still matter, but a custodian with no test attached is a name on a page, and names get reassigned quietly when people change roles. Dated output beats a recurring meeting.
