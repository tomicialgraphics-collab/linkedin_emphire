## Why ask, and why ask now

**Because the answer decides whether seven hours is well spent.** Two assessments and three references is roughly seven to nine hours before the deadline. If the programme cannot host a fellow who needs a visa, that time is spent on a place that cannot be taken, and the same seven hours have a use elsewhere.

**Because the programme has invited exactly this.** Their email says to make accommodation requests as early as possible. A question about entering the country is the same category of question as an accommodation, and it arrives with thirteen days on the clock rather than thirteen hours.

**Because a January start is already tight.** A visa appointment from Lagos, followed by processing, is a matter of weeks on a good run. If the programme does sponsor, the process needs to have started before the decision lands in December, not after.

**Why it is not a request for remote work.** It says in terms that the format is not the question. Asking two things at once, whether the format can bend and whether a visa exists, invites one answer to both, and the answer that matters is the second one.

## The risk, stated plainly

A question about sponsorship can mark a candidate as a cost before anyone has read the takehome. That is the trade: a small risk of being read as complicated now, against a large certainty of spending the week on an application that ends at a border. Take the small risk, and take it in a single short email that also says the work has already started.

## The same question is owed to the other two

Aithos is Amsterdam, with monthly visits written into the advertisement. Asymptote is New York, on site, and already carries the relocation question in its own DM. Three processes, one constraint, and it is cheaper to ask all three this week than to discover in December that the same answer applies to all of them. Aithos is the one already in conversation with a named person, so asking there costs one sentence in a thread that is open.
