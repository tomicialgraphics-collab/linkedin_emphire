## Message, paste as is

<!-- gate:body:start -->
Marvin, a question, and then something I keep turning over.

Of the twenty-four, which are blockchain work rather than next door to it? And of those, which one has a specification, something that runs, and no users yet? That last combination is the one I care about.

Here is why. I could keep building my own thing. I have one in mind and it is decent. But I am one person with no funding, and the honest risk is that it sits in a repository looking finished and never meets a user.

You have the opposite problem. More specification than a person could build alone, and no queue of customers.

So instead of building mine, I would rather put my weight behind one of yours. You stay the architect. I take it to the first ten users and carry those conversations. Not a job and not a favour. A partnership on one product, and if the first one works we look at the second.

I can't do all twenty-four and I won't pretend to. I know Lagos and a little of West Africa. I do not know the American market and I would be useless to you there.

You built all of this because you could not stop. I would like one of them to outlast the repository it is currently sitting in.

Tell me which one you would trust a stranger with, and I will start there.
<!-- gate:body:end -->

## Why the question has three conditions in it

Specification, something that runs, no users. Each one removes a category: a specification alone is an idea, something that runs without a specification cannot be handed to a second person, and a project that already has users is somebody else's business. What is left is the only kind of project that can be co-built by two people who met a week ago.

## Why the offer is stated as a partnership rather than a role

A role would make the owner a junior in a company of one. A partnership on one product gives both sides the thing they lack. It also keeps the conversation away from employment until there is a result worth arguing about.

## Why the limits are volunteered

He has fifty-five years of experience and can hear an overclaim in a sentence. Saying plainly that the American market is not available, and that twenty-four projects is not a plan, is what makes the rest of the message believable.

## Why the last line before the question is about time

It is the only sentiment in the message and it is the true one. A man of seventy-four who describes his own projects as fun does not need to be told his work matters. He needs one of them to be used by somebody who is not him.
