=== COMMENT 3 - REPLY TO: compliance / legal (factual question) ===
Their line: "Does this apply to all AI systems, or only
generative ones?"
Reply:
Only a specific set. Article 50's transparency duties land on
generative systems (mark the output machine-readable), chatbots
(say you're a bot), deepfakes (disclose), and text published to
inform the public on matters of public interest. If you're not
in one of those four you're mostly clear, but "generative" is
wider than people think. The repo walks the boundaries.
(WHY: precise scope, adds the "wider than people think" nudge,
hands the repo. Factual, no pitch.)
