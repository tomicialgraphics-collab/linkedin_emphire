Going public didn't change what Anthropic can build. It changed what it can claim.

The difference between a private and a public company isn't the money. It's the sentence. In private you can say we think this is probably true. In public that sentence carries a signature, and someone with a lawyer and a spreadsheet can ask for the workings.

That's the whole shift this week. Not capability. Evidence.

Which means the interesting question isn't what the models can do. It's what a lab can put in front of a stranger that shows what the model actually did, on which date, and with whose name against it. Most of them can't answer yet, and it isn't because anyone is lying. It's because nobody wrote it down while it was happening.

Every AI company is about to be asked the question that was just asked of one. The ones who can answer will look calm.

If you buy AI for a living, try this on your current vendor this week. Ask for one claim about their system that comes with evidence you could check without them in the room. Then watch how long the answer takes to arrive, because the delay tells you more than the answer will.
