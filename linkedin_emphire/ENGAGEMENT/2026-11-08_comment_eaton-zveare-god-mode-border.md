# Comment on Eaton Zveare's Black Hat abstract, 8 November 2026

## Why this one

Two reasons, and only one of them is the research. Nigeria is the first country in his list and this account is written
from Lagos, so there is a true and specific thing to say that almost nobody else in that thread can say.

The other reason is that his abstract ends with an availability notice. He is a Black Hat and DEF CON speaker saying he
will be looking for something new in API and web application security. That is worth being in the thread for, but the
comment must not read as an application, or it becomes the thing every recruiter in his notifications already sent.

## Comment

Nigeria is on that list, and I am writing this from Lagos. The part I will be listening for on the 10th is not the find. It
is the fix.

Every one of those six systems came from a contractor, and nothing in this bug class needs fresh research to come back. It
needs a new tender, and a team that never read the previous disclosure. So the durable artefact is the boring one: a
register of which systems exist, who built them, and which findings were remediated and verified rather than announced.

BOLA is a code problem. Whether it stays fixed is a procurement one, and that is not a talk anyone gives.

## Optional direct message, only if the owner decides to send it

Eaton, your abstract stopped me twice. Nigeria is on the list and I am in Lagos, and those six systems look like the same
pattern I meet from the other side now: agents holding API credentials that nobody deliberately granted.

If your next move touches AI-facing APIs, I would like to compare notes. I red team agentic products and spend most of
that time on what a refusal or a failure leaves behind. No pitch. A conversation after the 10th if the timing suits.

## What not to say

No congratulations, no "this is terrifying", no question about how he got in, because the abstract says the writeup lands
the same day and asking early is asking him to leak his own talk. No offer of work and no mention of availability, since
that is his line, not mine. No reference to any individual system or ministry, which would be both reckless and rude.

The word for a local test rig is avoided throughout and his employer is not named, because one is on the banned list and
the other is not the point.

## Held for a later exchange

His black box approach is the overlap worth developing: he takes an API he was never given and finds the boundary that was
never enforced, and agent work is the same problem with a model in the middle choosing which call to make. If he replies,
that is the thread.
