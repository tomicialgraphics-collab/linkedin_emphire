## How to fill it

- **Followers**: the number on the profile, same time each day, 20:00 WAT.

- **Best impression count**: the highest single number in the post or comment analytics that day.
- **What brought them**: the one source with the most follower growth attributed. If LinkedIn does not
  say, write "unknown" and put a name in the note below. Never guess in the table.
