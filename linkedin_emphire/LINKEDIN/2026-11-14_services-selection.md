# Services to select from the LinkedIn picker

The picker offers roughly a hundred options across fifteen categories. The current profile holds five, and two of them are the reason a security lead clicks away: Mobile Application Development and Career Development Coaching.

Select these ten. Remove the five already there first, if the cap is ten in total rather than ten additional.

## What to leave out, and why it matters

Everything in Finance, Real Estate, Law, Events, Design, Home Improvement and Accounting. Also leave out the five already-adjacent items that dilute the page: Web Development, Mobile Application Development, iOS Development, Android Development and Custom Software Development. Every one of them is a true statement about his skills and a false statement about what he is selling.

The rest of Coaching and Mentoring is the trap. Life Coaching, Career Development Coaching, Resume Review and Interview Preparation are the categories most commonly attached to people who are between jobs. They do not make a services page look busy. They make it look like a person who is not sure what he sells, and the profile already carries an open-to-work banner that says the same thing once.

## Each service needs a description

LinkedIn lets each one carry a short description and a call to action. Use the same one-line offer in all of them, so the page reads as one business:

"I break AI systems on purpose, then hand over the tests that keep the hole closed. Fixed scope, fixed price, and a report you can put in front of a client."

Where the service is training, change the second half: "run against your team, with the failures they are likely to ship."

## The test

Ten services that describe one business read as a specialist who is easy to buy from. Fifteen that describe four businesses read as an agency of one. The number matters less than the count of distinct things being sold, and that number should be one.
