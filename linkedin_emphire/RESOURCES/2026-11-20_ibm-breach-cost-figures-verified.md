## The denominator, which is the part that travels

The sample is **600 organisations across 16 countries and 17 industries**, fielded March 2024 to February 2025, and it is
self-reported. The breaches studied ran between roughly **2,960 and 113,620 records**, and IBM cautions against
multiplying the per-record figure across very large breaches. So the headline excludes the mega-breaches, which is
exactly the class a large bank or a government register would call a bad day.

## How to say it, if it is said at all

"The 2025 IBM study puts the global average at $4.44M and financial services at $5.56M, across 600 organisations and
breaches of up to about 114,000 records."

Naming the study, the year and the sample in one breath is the difference between a number a CFO can use and a number
they have to check. Do not write "for your vertical" unless the sector figure is being quoted by name.
