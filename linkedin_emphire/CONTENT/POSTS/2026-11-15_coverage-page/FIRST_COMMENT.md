The checklist and the coverage rule are public, at https://github.com/Teamthy/Ai-Red-Team-Playbook, under CC BY for the writing and MIT for the code.

Start with the coverage section, because it is the part most lists leave out. It states what the 168 does not cover, how the tiers fall, and the rule that stops a class disappearing quietly when an engagement runs out of days.

My own limit, in the open: 24 extended classes sit outside a two-week engagement by default. The one I would add first is coordination between subagents that spawn after a kill switch fires, because the switch stops the agent you can see.
