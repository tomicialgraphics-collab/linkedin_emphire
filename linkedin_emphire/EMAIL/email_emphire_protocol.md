## RULES (non-negotiable)

R1 CONSENT BEFORE CONTACT. Three capture points only, all
consent-based, same logic as the no-cold-DM rule in LINKEDIN:
  1. Agent Gate DM reply (LANTERN 3, step 3 context)
  2. Article 50 / article one-pager (end-of-article line + reply CTA)
  3. Warm comment reply (only where the conversation already
     touched a number or a claim)
Copy for all three lives in EMAIL/magnet_verification_receipt.md.
A reply is the consent. Never: cold email to a scraped address,
bought lists, a "newsletter pitch" to someone who never engaged.
Every contact is logged in the CONTACTS LOG below with: date, name,
source, consent timestamp, the exact consent line used, tier
(decision-maker or practitioner room).

R2 NO OFFER BEFORE THE GATE. Stage 1 emails never mention the Agent
Gate, the price, or the slots. The only CTAs allowed: reply (a reply
is consent for the next step), read, run. Stage 2 copy exists but is
HELD in EMAIL/sequence_02_offer.md. Gate = 100+ contacts AND 2+
receipts delivered AND 1+ receipt reply, then the owner says GO.

R3 NO DASH PUNCTUATION. No em dash, no en dash, no spaced hyphen as a
sentence separator, in any email, subject line, or footer. Use
commas, colons, periods, parentheses. Hyphens inside compound words
(re-runnable, 48-hour, one-page, decision-maker) are unaffected.

R4 BANNED WORDS APPLY. banned_words.txt at repo root. No delve, no
leverage, no unlock, no robust, no streamline, no harness, no
navigate, no landscape, no elevate, no comprehensive, no "it's worth
noting", no "in today's world". If a banned word is the only word
that fits, the sentence is rewritten, not the word swapped.

R5 MOTIF BUDGET. The brand motifs (re-run, receipt, claim not a
result) are allowed at max 1 to 2 uses per email, and they must vary
across the sequence. A sequence where every email lands on the same
line is a template, and templates get unsubscribed.

R6 ONE IDEA, ONE CTA. Every email carries one idea and at most one
CTA. The CTA is a reply, a read, or a run. No email ends with a
method. E1 to E2 are method-first (the receipt is the method, that
is the proof). E3 to E5 are consequence-first (forcing functions,
dated triggers, the decision room). E6 is the qualifier.

R7 TWO REGISTERS APPLY. Same rule as the LINKEDIN division: classify
before writing. PRACTITIONER room gets method-first. DECISION-MAKER
room gets consequence-first, names the forcing function (board,
insurer, questionnaire, diligence, the 2 December marking grace,
2027 Annex III), method only as a one-sentence answer to "how would
you know?", closes on the decision or the date. The sequence arc is
built so both rooms get their register in the right email.

R8 REAL SCARCITY ONLY. Two receipts a month is real (it is the
delivery capacity). Two Agent Gate slots a month is real (same
reason). No fake countdowns, no invented deadlines, no "last chance".
If the limit is not real, it is not in the email.

R9 EVERY EMAIL CARRIES THE FOOTER. Sender identity, what this note
is, the source line (why they are on it), the one-click unsubscribe,
Lagos address and timezone. No footer, no send.

R10 RECORD SENDS ONLY AFTER THE OWNER SAYS SENT. Same discipline as
the LINKEDIN log: DRAFTED until sent, SENT on the owner's word,
OPENED/REPLIED on data. Nothing is marked delivered from hope.

R11 THE P-GROUP TEST (Suby, the email chapter). Every send must
look personal or it does not send. Plain text only, no HTML, no
images, no logo in the header. Personal sender name and address
(notes@ or sales@ never). Subject 1 to 20 characters, lowercase,
curiosity, no formula ("7 secrets to X" is banned, it causes email
blindness). First line of the body is the contact's first name plus
their context (the personalization engine, below). Preheader is a
mid-sentence hook, never the whole story. One CTA, written as a
command, not a question ("reply with the date", not "could you
reply with the date?").

R12 GOODWILL 2/3 (Suby). Two thirds of all sends are pure value
(the weekly research, the onboarding value). At most one third
carry an offer, and the offer lives in the P.S. only, never in the
first two lines. The body must stand alone as a valuable piece of
content. Stage 1 P.S. is the receipt line (the magnet, not a sale).

R13 THE WEEKLY VERIFIED RESEARCH IS THE SPINE. One verified
research email a week (the W), every week, to everyone on the list
plus the onboarding layer. "Verified" means the re-run was run and
the fingerprint logged, not that the number was cited, and every
item states who ran what (agent re-run, or verified to source with
the live re-run flagged as an owner job). The agent produces the
VERIFIED LIST (2 to 4 receipts) each slot; the owner scans it and
picks one; the pick is the weekly send (weekly pipeline, below).
If no candidate passes the quality gate that week, the honest
week email goes out instead (short, true, cadence kept). The
research loop spec lives in the playbook, section 3.

R14 PERSONALIZED WEEKLY, HONESTLY. Three layers: (1) the first line
is merged from the contact record (name + their context, 1 to 3
real facts, Hormozi L12); (2) the body is one of two framings of
the same research (practitioner method-first, decision-maker
consequence-first, per R7); (3) every reply enters the 1:1 lane,
hand-written same day, never batched, the only lane that carries
the offer (after the gate). At 500, layers 1 and 2 are templated
personalization and layer 3 is hand. That is what personalized
means at this scale, and it is stated in the log, not faked.

## WEEKLY PIPELINE (roles and handoffs, owner workflow 2026-10-27)

The owner directed the operating rhythm: the agent runs the
research, the owner scans the verified list, then the agent gives
the personalized email for each contact. Four steps, two roles.

STEP 1 (agent): RUN THE RESEARCH. Run the weekly research loop
(candidate pool, quality gate, the re-runs, the fingerprints).
Output: the VERIFIED LIST, 2 to 4 items, each a full receipt
(claim, result, method, honest line), the fingerprint, a stated
who-ran-what line (what the agent ran versus what is flagged as
an owner job), the two framings (practitioner, decision-maker),
the subject options (1 to 20 chars), the why-this-week, and the
owner scan checklist. File: EMAIL/research/
YYYY-MM-DD_slotN_verified_list.md. Hand to the owner.

STEP 2 (owner): SCAN THE LIST. Check the inputs (the scan
checklist per item), pick one item (or adjust a line, or pass to
the honest week). Nothing sends before the owner's pick. Record
the pick in the file (PICK line).

STEP 3 (agent): PERSONALIZED SENDS. After the pick, generate the
personalized email for each contact on the list, one per
contact, in EMAIL/sends/YYYY-Www.md (created at the first send):
- Layer 1, the first line: merged from the contact record
  (R14). For contacts with a LinkedIn (all current captures do),
  use their actual thread, post, or role, 1 to 3 real facts
  (Hormozi L12). For email-only contacts, the source line.
- Layer 2, the framing: practitioner or decision-maker first
  paragraph, per the contact's room (R7).
- The shared body: the four lines, the honest line, the teaser
  (if real), the P.S. (R12), the footer (R9).
Hand the send file to the owner. Nothing is queued before the
owner's read-through.

STEP 4 (owner): SEND. Send or queue per contact. Mark SENT (R10).
Log opens and replies in the KPI table. Every reply enters the
1:1 lane (hand-written, same day, R14 layer 3).

File conventions: research lists in EMAIL/research/ (one file per
slot, permanent record). Personalized sends in EMAIL/sends/ (one
file per send week, per-contact blocks with state: DRAFTED,
SENT, REPLIED). The research file is where the verified list
lives for the owner to scan; the sends file is where the
personalized output lives for the owner to approve.

## PLATFORM SETUP (free tier, platform-agnostic)

No platform yet (owner, 2026-10-27). Setup order:
1. SENDING DOMAIN. A custom domain or subdomain (notes.yourdomain).
   Never a free mailbox address for a growing list. SPF, DKIM,
   DMARC records set at signup.
2. DOUBLE OPT-IN. ON. The list is foreign-first (foreign_first_pivot
   2026-10-25), so EU contacts are in scope: GDPR. Double opt-in plus
   a stored consent record (R1) is the floor. US: CAN-SPAM
   (identity, address, opt-out honored. Immediate is the practice).
3. HOSTED vs OWNED. Option A, start: a hosted free tier (verify
   current limits at signup, they move. Resend, Brevo, Mailchimp
   class). Fine to 300 to 500 contacts. Option B, the empire
   standard from ~500: listmonk, self-hosted on a $5 VPS. No
   per-recipient cost, full ownership, the email is the asset so the
   infrastructure is owned too. Decide at 300, not at 5,000.
4. WARM-UP. Week 1: 10 to 20 sends/day. Ramp roughly 2x per week to
   100/day. Never blast the free tier on day one. Reputation is the
   channel.
5. UNSUBSCRIBE. One click, immediate, no confirmation step, no
   guilt trip in the goodbye.
6. FOOTER. Per R9, on every send, including the opt-in
   confirmation.

## KPIs (weekly, logged in the KPI table below)

ENGAGED (the score, Hormozi L1): replies plus receipt requests,
count. A reply moves the person to the 1:1 lane, hand-written,
same day. The list is where trust is banked, not where the sale
happens.
LIST: verified count against the 500 target (the 500 plan,
playbook section 4), net new by lane (0 seed, 1 buyer pipeline,
2 content, 3 referral), unsubscribes, gate progress (100
contacts / 2 receipts delivered / 1 receipt reply)
DELIVERED: bounce and complaint rates (the first of Suby's three
things; sender score check monthly, playbook section 6)
OPEN: percent (warm consent list benchmark 35 to 55. Under 25
after 5 sends = subject problem, rewrite subjects, keep bodies.
Hygiene, not the score)
RECEIPTS: requested, scheduled, delivered, referral line sent
UNSUB: percent per send (over 2 on any send = the note is not
delivering, check research quality before the next send)

## KILL / HOLD RULES (SLC discipline, from the playbook)

- 0 replies in 50 touches: HOLD all sends, audit the capture
  sources. A list that does not reply was not consented with a
  reason.
- Open under 20 percent after 5 sends: subject lines are the
  problem. Rewrite, keep the bodies.
- The reply rate is the number. Open rate is vanity here. This list
  is a sales trust list, not a media list.
- Any reply: the person leaves the batch lane and enters 1:1. Never
  run a campaign at someone who has talked to you.
- Base-case velocity (500 plan) under 15 verified a week for three
  weeks: the magnet is the problem before the message is (Hormozi
  L13). Change the magnet wording or the capture reason, not the
  touch volume.
- Three consecutive non-opens per contact: quiet list, monthly
  sends (sender reputation is driven by engagement, Suby E-3).
  Quiet list contacts are still on the list, just at a lower
  cadence.
