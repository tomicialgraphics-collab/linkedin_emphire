# Comment on Soubhik Ghosh's interview communication post, 6 November 2026

## Draft, paste as a comment, 139 words

All three land, and the dry run is the one that separates people who have shipped from people who have
practised.

One addition from the reviewing side. Most people who lose marks on "think out loud" are narrating
code instead of decisions. What gets scored is the branch: the constraint you noticed, the two
approaches you weighed, the one you rejected and why. Say the rejected one out loud and you have shown
judgement rather than typing speed.

The other skill that never makes these lists: saying you do not know, then how you would find out. On
a real team that instinct beats a fast answer, because it is the same one that stops somebody guessing
in production.

An interview is a sample, not a measurement. Behave like the sample will be taken again under a
different constraint.
