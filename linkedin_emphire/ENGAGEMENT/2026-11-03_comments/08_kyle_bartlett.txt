Mine compares against the archive rather than the draft: a banned phrase bank, every piece already published, and the per-recipient history, so the same closing line can't go to two people in one day. The failure I keep running into is the log itself. When the write that records what went out fails, the check compares against an empty set, finds nothing repeated, and passes. That is your case one layer down: the reader is fine, the thing it reads is missing, and the first person to notice is the recipient. So I check that the log grew, not just that the draft passed.
