agent learns whatever it is graded on, and you replaced one grade with
three better ones. Margin on the account after refund is the fragile
one; it teaches the agent the smallest refund that keeps the customer
buying, not the refund that is right. The scorecard degrades the way

after rule, scorecard, reason, approver, time, in the same record as the
refunds. Then the investigation flips from 'why did the agent change' to
'is there an approved change for this behavior', and behavior that moved
without a recorded approval is the incident, not the refund.

Your test, 'who defined worked', is the right question for the agent.

where the value is. The held-back number is the alarm; the approval line

  Your third question is the measurement. Attention isn't something you
  poll the user for; it's something you count from the log. Every
  escalation, every handoff the person had to coordinate, every task

  time-saved promise, and the record is what tells you the drop is real
  rather than a quieter week.
