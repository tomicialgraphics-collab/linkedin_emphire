Then you already know the thing I keep running into. The SE wins the room, the AE gets paid, and a founder has to be both in the same hour with the same person.

Here's what I'd add, having watched a few of these. The SE's best tool is disqualification. The ones worth trusting can look at a buyer's stack and say this won't work for you, in the meeting, before anyone has polished anything. It looks like losing a deal. It's how a forecast stays honest.

That you niched down to AE and still answered my objection with a question instead of a slide is the whole thing, really. A pure number on a slide gets attacked. You wrote an ask, because you've been in the room when a number got taken apart.

For us it's simple for now. I'm the SE, because there's nothing true to demonstrate yet. The AE part gets a lot easier the moment there is.
