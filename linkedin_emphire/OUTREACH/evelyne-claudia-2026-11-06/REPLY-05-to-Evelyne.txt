The second one went against me. Comparing complete host records rather than kinds, the first run reports them as

with both cells carrying the same action id, the records are identical in every field. So the sentence about the
host failing to separate the two refusals was broader than any measurement I had, and it is now licensed only for
two runs carrying the same action id. Both runs ship, and the one that fails is there for a reason.

The count is renamed to acknowledged verdict requests and kept apart from the durable records: one against none
acknowledged, two against none durable. The first is what the store told the caller it had accepted. The second is
what survives a restart and can be put in front of an auditor.
