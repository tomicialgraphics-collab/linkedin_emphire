The eccentric critiques are the ones worth writing down before acting on them. A substantive critique reads as correct before it has been checked, and the odd-sounding ones are where you find out whether it is. What I do now is keep each objection tied to a line and to a version of the document, so the claim can be tested against the text instead of against my memory of reading it. The ones that survive are usually the best feedback the draft gets. The ones that don't are usually the ones that sounded sharpest on first reading.
