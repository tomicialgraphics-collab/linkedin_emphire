# Profile conversion audit, 14 November 2026

Read from six screenshots of the live profile, taken at 1,243 followers with 596 profile views, 11,055 post impressions and 11 search appearances in the past seven days.

## Verdict

Retain the profile. Convert four surfaces on it. Nothing here needs a rebuild, and the assets people pay for are already present: a named recommendation from someone with a corpus attached, three Featured artifacts, a banner that states a position, and a feed that reaches five figures.

What is wrong is narrower and worse. The profile is selling four unrelated things, and two of them are mobile app development and career coaching.

## The diagnostic that matters

Eleven search appearances in a week is the tell. People are not finding this profile when they look for someone who does this work. Post impressions were 11,055 in the same window, so the traffic exists and the discoverability does not. Search is a keyword problem, and keywords are set in the headline, About, skills and Services.

Second tell: 596 profile views against 11,055 impressions is a 5.4% view rate, which is healthy. The problem is not the number of people who arrive. It is what is standing there when they do.

## Four surfaces to change, in order of effect

### 1. Services: delete five of them

Currently listed: Mobile Application Development, Web Development, Custom Software Development, Career Development Coaching, Training.

A security lead who clicks through from a post about truncated hashes reads that list and concludes they are on the wrong profile. Replacing it costs nothing and changes what the page says about who is for sale. Suggested:

- AI red teaming
- AI security assessment
- LLM evaluation
- Security review and evidence audit

Keep Training only if the workshops are actually sold. Career coaching is the one to remove first.

### 2. Headline: lead with the outcome, not the job title

Current: "Software Engineer | AI Red Teaming | Breaking AI products before users do | Web · AI Integration | Teaching engineers to ship things that don't break"

It opens with the most generic word available, then lists five things, two of which belong to a different business. Suggested:

"I break AI systems before your users do | AI red teaming, evals and verification for teams shipping agents | Go, Python, adversarial evidence"

That is 138 characters, keeps the strongest existing line, names the three things he actually does, and carries the two keywords people search for. "Teaching engineers" can live in About where it has room to mean something.

### 3. About: the first two lines are the whole page

Current opening, which is all a visitor sees before the fold: "I trained as a Medical Laboratory Scientist. Now I break AI systems on purpose."

The medical laboratory line is a good story and the wrong door. A buyer gives the About four seconds and stops at the first sentence they cannot use. Suggested opening:

"I find the failure in AI systems that a passing test suite says cannot happen. Twenty years of shipping software, two of them spent attacking models, agents and evaluations on purpose.

Recent work: I found that my own verifier compared 16 of the SHA-256 characters instead of 64 and passed every check, then rebuilt it so it fails when it should. An independent reviewer found the same class of gap in the same week."

The medical line goes third, where it reads as texture rather than thesis.

### 4. Skills: the top five are the search index

Listed top skills: Software Systems Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Go, Next.js, Red Teaming.

Next.js is not a skill anyone hires for in this market and it is occupying a search slot. Pin, in this order: AI Evaluation, AI red teaming, LLM security, Threat modeling, Go. Note that "ai red teaming" is currently lowercase and inconsistent with "AI Evaluation" above it, which looks careless on a page that sells rigour.

## Featured: change the order, not the contents

Retain all four. Reorder to put the Playbook first, because it is the only item that shows work rather than selling it, then the fifteen-point scan, then the design review, then a post.

One line to fix. The featured post card reads "I published the checklist I use for AI red teaming, 168 classes" which is the author announcing his own activity, the same opening that produced 77 impressions on a Playbook launch while an event-anchored post produced 8,001. Rewrite the card text as a claim: "168 testable classes, with the tooling to turn a run into a coverage argument someone else can check." That sentence already exists on the card underneath and is doing the work the headline should do.

## Smaller items, each five minutes

- **Verify the identity badge.** "Verify in 2 minutes" is sitting in the open. For remote clients and for a US relocating employer, a verified badge is free trust.
- **Open to work, recruiters only.** Fine to keep for the Asymptote and Anthropic applications. It conflicts with a services page only if a buyer sees it, and recruiters-only means they mostly will not.
- **Experience overlap.** OpenCV to November 2025 and Teamart from March 2024 are both full-time and both remote, with two years of overlap. A recruiter reads that as either a part-time arrangement labelled full-time or a copy error. Either consolidate or say in the blurb that the practice ran alongside.
- **Interests.** Brian Wong and Gary Vaynerchuk at the top of the Interests section tells a visitor this profile reads growth content. Replace with AI security people, or remove the section.
- **Banner.** The two photographs compete with the words and the small "Let's break it first" bubble is unreadable at feed size. One photograph, the claim, and the three domains is stronger than two faces.

## What not to change

The recommendation stays exactly as it is, including the amended scope sentence, because it is the only third-party statement on the page and it is specific. The Featured artifacts stay, including both Upwork cards, because routing views to Upwork is the stated strategy. The posting cadence stays, because 11,055 impressions in a week is working.

## The test after the change

One week from now, look at two numbers and nothing else. Search appearances should move off 11, and profile views should hold above 500. If search does not move, the keywords are still wrong and the headline is the place to fix it.
