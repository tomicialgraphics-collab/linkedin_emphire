# Skip: Charly Wargnier's Jensen petition

## The decision

No comment, no reaction, no repost. If a signal of support is wanted later, it goes in a private message and not under the post.

## Why

**The thread contains an unsourced allegation about a named person.** A reply 20 minutes in says the question is whether Jensen Huang "crossed, or pushed close, to the line of giving in to foreign interests." That is an accusation about a living executive's conduct, offered without a source. Anything written under it sits inside it. A careful sentence next to a reckless one reads as consent to the pair, and no reply is careful enough to survive being screenshotted with the comment above it.

**Nothing in the lane can be added.** The work is about checks that can fail and numbers that can be reproduced. A petition is advocacy. There is no measurement to make, no claim to test, and the only contribution available is a vote, which is what signing is for and what the post is asking for.

**It would contaminate the piece already planned for Monday.** The 17 November post is about NVIDIA's Q3 FY2027 call, the first without him, and it needs to read as an analyst reading a company rather than a participant in a campaign. A comment tonight makes the Monday post look like the same act in a longer form, and the audience for that post includes people who will check.

## What to do instead

If NVIDIA's board and the leadership change are worth writing about, write about the earnings call on Monday with numbers in it. The line that survives scrutiny is the one that never mentions the petition.

## What would change this

A published source for the allegation, from a party with standing to make it. At that point it stops being a comment thread and becomes a story, and a story still gets written in his own post rather than under someone else's.
