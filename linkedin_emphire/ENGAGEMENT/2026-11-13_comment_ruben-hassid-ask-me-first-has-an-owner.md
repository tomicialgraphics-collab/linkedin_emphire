## Comment, paste as is

Ask me first moves the decision, it does not settle it. Every approval it generates lands on one person, and that person is the least available component in the system: asleep, on leave, or answering fast at the end of a long day. A rule that depends on me being reachable is a rule that stops holding the first week I am not.

What I would write instead is a rule with a reason and an owner inside it. Prices come from the finance sheet, so changing one needs whoever owns that sheet, and here is why the rule exists in the first place. That survives a model upgrade, a new colleague and my holiday, and it tells the agent which person to ask rather than whoever is in the chair.

Keep the approvals you have already given, too. A month of yes, that kind of change is fine, written down, is a policy that builds itself, and it is the only route I know where the interruptions actually fall over time.
