Section 5 of the measurement chapter in the playbook is the version of this I keep coming back to: a judge is an instrument, so you calibrate it before you trust its numbers. A verifier is the same kind of object, and I'd been treating mine as exempt.

The rule I took out of it: before any result counts, prove the check can return a different answer. Feed it a mutation you know is wrong. If it still passes, the number it produced was never a measurement.

It's public, CC BY for the writing and MIT for the code, at https://github.com/Teamthy/Ai-Red-Team-Playbook for anyone who wants the checklist rather than the anecdote.
