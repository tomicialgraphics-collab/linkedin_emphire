The price tag line is the one I keep thinking about.

Before this week a handful of people valued Anthropic once or twice a year, and they got to sit in the room and ask questions. Now millions of people reprice it every day, and none of them get the room.

So the price can only move on what outsiders can check. Revenue they can check. Whether the models actually do what the lab says they do, mostly they can't, not yet.

My guess is that second gap moves the price before revenue does.
