You said you'd like us to write a PRD for my project. ## Why the first line is an instruction followed, not a thank you

He asked to be told when he is not being understood. Thanking him and then writing another dense message would make the thanks meaningless. Asking the question in plain words, in the next breath, is the proof that the offer was taken.

## Why the ambiguity is named rather than resolved by guessing

Two subjects are live and they need different documents. Guessing wastes his time on the wrong one, and asking a direct question is the behaviour he just asked for. Naming it now also quietly makes the point that the owner has not dropped his own idea in order to flatter him.

## Why the testing line is in this message

He offered to help build. The line makes plain that the owner's contribution is not only construction, because a spec can be built by anybody with a subscription and tested by almost nobody.

## Why it ends on his children

He made a joke about his five sons and the owner's father. Answering it once, briefly, keeps the exchange human, and doing it at the end means the message closes warm rather than on a request.
