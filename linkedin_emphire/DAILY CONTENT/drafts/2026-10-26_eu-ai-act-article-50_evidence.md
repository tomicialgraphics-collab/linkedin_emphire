POST TEXT (recommended headline + subheadline + body, copy from

On 2 December, "this is AI" stops being a sentence your team
gets to say.

Article 50 of the EU AI Act has applied since 2 August. The
grace period for marking generative output closes in five weeks,
and penalties run to 15 million euros. And the requirement is
not what most teams think it is.

It doesn't ask you to keep a log. A log is something your team
writes down and hopes is believed. It's an archive, and an
archive proves nothing to the person holding the output.

It asks you to make the output checkable. Generative systems
have to mark what they produce, machine-readable, so anyone
with the output can re-run the detection and see the signal for
themselves. Not read your note. Run the check.

That's the whole thing. It's the same split an auditor draws
between two records that look identical on a shelf: one you can
re-perform, one you can only read. The first one is evidence.
The second one is a story you tell about evidence.

Most teams will read this as "add a disclosure footer" and stop.
A footer is a claim. The mark is a check. By 2 December, the
gap between those two words is the gap between a system that
passes inspection and one that fails on the first question
asked.

The log is what you keep. The mark is what you can prove.
