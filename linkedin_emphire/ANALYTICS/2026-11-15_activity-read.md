# What the activity page says, 15 November

## The one number that matters

Four comments account for almost all of the reach on this page.

| comment on | impressions |
|---|---|
| Love Babbar, CEO succession | 21,516 |
| Jess Ramos, the Huang ethics post | 6,155 |
| Sam Illingworth, the AI tutor study | 3,159 |
| Elena Bobkova, the security summit question | 998 |

That is **31,828 impressions from four comments**. Everything else on the page, thirty-odd pieces of writing including every post of his own, sits between 2 and 405.

His own posts, in order of when they went out: 129, 48, 33, 27, 10, 6, 5, 5, 4, 3, 3, 2, 2. The 168-class post, which is the most substantial thing he has published this week, reached **four people** in one comment thread of its own and **five** in the other.

## What that means

**The writing is working. The distribution is not.** A comment under a post with 768 likes is shown to that post's audience. The 21,516 is not his audience; it is Love Babbar's, borrowed for a day. His own posts go out to 1,279 followers and reach a fraction of them, because a post with no engagement in the first hour is not shown again.

**Twelve comments a day is spreading the same effort across threads that do not compound.** Three or four comments landed on the four large accounts and did all the work. The other thirty were written as well and reached a few dozen people each.

**Nothing on this page converts.** Impressions are not profile views, and neither is a sale. What the page cannot tell him is whether the 21,516 people who saw a comment about authority to refuse clicked anything. That number lives on the analytics page and it is the one worth checking before writing another comment.

## The free fix, and it is live now

One comment carries a dead repository URL. On the post beginning "The verifier printed NOT TESTED and exited 0", posted two days ago, the reply reads:

> Everything lives in https://github.com/Teamthy/**Ai-Rep-Team-Playbook** if you want to read it, and nothing in it asks you to star anything.

The repository is `Teamthy/Ai-Red-Team-Playbook`. **Rep** is not **Red**, so that link 404s for every reader who tried it. Every other mention on the page has it right.

Edit that comment. It is two minutes, it is the only fixable error on the page, and it is attached to the post that is least likely to be seen, which is the only reason it has not cost more.

## What I would do next, in order

**One. Check profile views before writing anything else.** If 21,516 impressions produced no lift in profile views, borrowed reach is not converting and the next three days should not repeat the pattern.

**Two. Take the two threads that worked and publish the version he owns.** The Love Babbar comment ends on "who is allowed to refuse, and whether anyone else has ever done it out loud". That is a post, and the case study and the playbook both exist to sit under it. The difference from the 168-class post is that this one already has an audience that engaged with the idea in someone else's thread.

**Three. Cut the volume and concentrate.** Two or three comments a day, chosen for audience size and topic fit, beat twelve spread evenly. The page is evidence for that, not an opinion about it.

No engagement rate, no follower change, no conversion figure. The paste does not contain them and inventing them would be the exact failure the account argues against.
