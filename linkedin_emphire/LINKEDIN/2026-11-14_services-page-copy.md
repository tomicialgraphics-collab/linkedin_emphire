## Pricing: leave it on Contact for pricing

The two options are a starting hourly rate and contact for pricing. Every offer here is scoped and priced up front, from the fifteen-point scan upwards. An hourly rate invites a buyer to multiply it against a project they cannot scope and arrive at a number lower than the quote. Contact for pricing matches what is actually sold, and the Featured cards already show the numbers for anyone who wants a starting point.

## Why it opens that way

The first line is the offer, not the biography, because the services page is read by someone deciding whether to send a message. The second line names the defect the buyer half-suspects about their own evaluations, which is the only thing that makes a stranger keep reading. The third line is evidence with a number in it, and the last line removes the three questions that stop a message being sent: what does it cost, how long, and where are you.
