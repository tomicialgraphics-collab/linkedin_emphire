why the binary question does not block the pack
  run.py and verify.py import nothing outside the standard library, so every number in
  02 and 03 was produced by the two files named in the reproduction section of 00-README.md,
  on this interpreter, with no network beyond loopback.

determinism
  the seed, the policy digest and the decision key are constants in bench/common.py
  the clock is a counter, not a wall clock, so artifacts carry no timestamps
  two runs of the same revision on this machine were compared file by file: 23 of 24 files
  byte-identical, and the 24th is port.txt, which records the ephemeral port the store
  bound to and is not evidence about anything
