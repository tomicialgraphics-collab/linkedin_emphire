# Yes, the buyer needs work. The ICP does not.

The ICP is already written and it is good. `STRATEGY/2026-10-31_icp-goals_push-plan.md` defines a security or
engineering leader at a 20 to 500 person company that has shipped an AI feature, found by dated public triggers, with 54
named targets and five published email addresses. That document is more specific than most agencies ever get.

The problem is not definition. It is that **three different buyers are being served at once**, and each one is being
served by a different surface, so none of them gets a coherent message.

## The three, side by side

| | ICP-A, the paying ICP | The catalog buyer | Who actually arrives |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who | CISO, VP Security, Head of AI Risk at 20 to 500 people | founder or engineering lead with one AI feature | 24 percent founders of very small software companies, 17 percent students, 8 percent security vendors |
| Price | $2,000 to $7,000 | $150 to $2,500 | none yet |
| What they want | a document they can hand an auditor | a test before a launch or a client questionnaire | to read, mostly |
| How they are reached | LinkedIn DM, five emails, 54 names | Upwork search and the catalog listing | comments on other people's posts |
| Where it is written down | the 31 October ICP plan | the two catalog listings | the profile and Featured section |
| Money it can produce by 25 December | little, at this throughput | one contract, with a payment rail already built | none |

The last row is the decision.

## The pick, for the next 42 days

**The catalog buyer, on Upwork.** Not because it is the better ICP. Because it is the only one with a checkout, a price
already published, an escrow mechanism, and a buyer who arrives intending to pay somebody this month.

ICP-A stays warm and unchanged. It cannot be worked to a December deadline with five email addresses and one DM lane, and
pretending otherwise is how the last eight strategy documents happened.

## The definition that needs sharpening

The catalog buyer is currently defined by what they have, an AI feature. That describes everybody and sells to nobody.
Define it by **what just happened**, and the copy writes itself:

> A founder or engineering lead at a 5 to 50 person company that shipped, or is about to ship, an LLM feature into a
> product with paying customers, and who has one of these three dates in front of them: an enterprise prospect sending a
> security questionnaire, a funding round with diligence, or a launch they cannot move.

Three triggers, each one dated, each one producing a reason to buy this month. A person with a trigger does not need
convincing that testing is a good idea; they need the price and the date.

## The four things this changes

1. **The first line of the listing speaks to the founder's fear, not the CISO's.** Not "does your AI feature resist
   injection", which is a security professional's question, but "someone is about to ask you for evidence about this
   feature and you do not have any". That is the sentence a founder reads and recognises.
2. **The proposal opener names the trigger and the date, not the technology.** "You are shipping in three weeks and
   somebody is going to ask what happens if a user tells it to ignore its instructions." Same offer, addressed to the
   person who has the deadline.
3. **The Featured cards stay as they are for fourteen days.** They were changed today and a rewrite before any data is
   the exact mistake this document exists to prevent.
4. **The research ICP stops borrowing the sales language.** Method, sample size, limitations belong to the research
   buyer; price, date and deliverable belong to the founder. Right now both are mixed into the same paragraphs, which is
   why the listings read like a paper abstract with a price on it.

## What would make this wrong

If thirty days pass with the catalog at more than fifty views and no enquiry, the buyer is not the constraint and the
offer is. If enquiries arrive and none close, the price is the constraint. If the profile continues to attract 17 percent
students, the arrival channel is the constraint, and no amount of ICP work fixes a channel that delivers the wrong room.

Three separate failures with three separate fixes, and today none of them has been measured long enough to tell which one
is running.
