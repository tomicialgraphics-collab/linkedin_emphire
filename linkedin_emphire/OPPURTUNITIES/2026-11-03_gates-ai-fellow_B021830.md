# Gates Foundation, AI Fellow, Africa Regional Offices: B021830

Found while checking the lead from 3 November. Deadline **11 November 2026**, eight days out, which is
the most useful thing in this file.

## The facts

- **Position:** AI Fellow, Africa Regional Offices. First Africa-based cohort of the AI Fellows Programme.
- **Requisition:** B021830.
- **Duration:** twelve months, full time, limited-term employment.
- **Duty stations:** Abuja, Nairobi, Addis Ababa, Johannesburg. Abuja is listed and Lagos is not.
- **Work:** one or two high-priority projects using frontier AI, machine learning and large language
  models on problems in global health, development and education across Sub-Saharan Africa. Named areas
  include decision-support tools, public financial management, and agricultural systems. Mentorship from
  AI, health, development and policy people.
- **Deadline:** 11 November 2026.

## The requirements, and the one that decides it

- Bachelor's or master's in computer science, data science, engineering, applied mathematics or related.
- At least two years applying AI solutions, preferably in health, public financial management or
  agriculture.
- Strong grounding in modern AI and machine learning, **model evaluation**, hands-on LLM work with
  prompting, retrieval, fine-tuning or deployment, plus software and data skills.
- Legal right to work in the duty-station country, without visa sponsorship. No relocation support.
  Background check.

**The gate is the degree.** The posting asks for a completed bachelor's or master's and his is in progress.
That is a real filter and it is the thing to resolve first: either the form accepts a candidate who is
finishing, or it does not, and no amount of artifact quality changes a screen that requires the parchment.
Ask the recruitment contact directly rather than guessing, because the eight-day window is long enough to
get an answer.

**The second condition is location, and here it is favourable rather than hostile.** Abuja is in Nigeria.
Working there needs no sponsorship, which is the condition that rules him out of most US and UK postings.
Relocation support is not provided, so Lagos to Abuja is his own move and his own cost, and he should say
plainly whether he would make it. Do not assume the answer, and do not apply into a duty station he cannot
hold for twelve months, which is the same failure mode flagged in the Anthropic and AIRT applications.

## Why this fits better than the NVIDIA role

The NVIDIA posting wants five or more years and a master's or PhD, and pays accordingly. This one wants two
years, a bachelor's, and evidence of applied AI work with evaluation in it. It is the stage he is actually
at, in the country he is actually in, and the evaluation requirement reads like a description of the work
he has already published. It is also a first cohort, which means the competition is a national pool rather
than a global one.

## What the application should lead with

1. **Measured failure, not claimed failure.** The twelve-day run across eighteen models, with defect
   findings published about the benchmark rather than only the scores.
2. **The review that found the defects in someone else's suite.** Model evaluation is a named requirement
   and this is the strongest single artifact for it.
3. **Five reproducible checks with raw outputs.** Public, runnable, and the opposite of a trust-me result.
4. **Lagos to Abuja.** Said once, plainly, with a yes.

## Order

1. Ask the recruiter whether a candidate completing a degree is eligible. One message, today.
2. If yes, build the pack: CV summary, two or three short answers, and the artifact links. Deadline is
   11 November, so a careful pack is possible rather than a rushed one.
3. If no, keep the relationship and ask about the next cohort.

Do not let the NVIDIA deadline today crowd this out. That application costs an hour and the odds are long;
this one costs the same hour and the conditions finally line up.
