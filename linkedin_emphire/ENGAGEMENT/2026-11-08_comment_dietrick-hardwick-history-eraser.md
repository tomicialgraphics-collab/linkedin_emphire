# Comment on Dietrick Hardwick's repost, 8 November 2026

## Why this one

He is the closest thing on this account to a fellow specialist rather than an audience. His whole subject is provenance,
which is the same subject as this week's work with different words, and he is a first degree connection who posts his own
thinking rather than links.

His repost has a joke structure, so the comment should not scold it. It should take the joke seriously for two sentences
and return something he can use. The line about recognising the value of what it excludes is the entry point: exclusion is
exactly the thing that leaves no trace, and it is his field rather than mine.

## Comment

The button's real property isn't that it erases. It's that after it's pressed, nothing in the machine can still say it was
pressed. Any record can be deleted cleanly enough that the deletion leaves no line of its own.

Which is the whole problem with a witness that lives inside the system it witnesses. It can testify about everything
except the one event that removes its ability to testify. Provenance at that edge isn't about what the system holds, it is
whether the system can still say what it no longer holds, and when that changed.

## What not to say

No political argument about the regulation post he is quoting, since that is not what he wrote and it would make the
thread about the article. No explanation of the history eraser reference back to him. Nothing about my own work, because
the observation stands on its own and his readers did not ask.

## Held for a later conversation

His Witness Protocol piece is the natural opening for a real exchange, and the honest question is whether a witness
outside the system is enough when the system can rewrite what the witness reads. That is a week's conversation, not a
comment, and it is the one worth having with him directly given he is already a connection.
