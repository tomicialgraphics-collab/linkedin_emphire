The 10 per cent is the interesting part now. A private company can hedge at 10 per cent. A public one can't, because
the numbers carry a signature and somebody outside can ask for the workings.

That's the change today, and it isn't about what the models can do. It's that claims about what they did stop being
narrative and become things a stranger has to be able to check, later, without trusting the team that made them.

Which means the first question in a board room probably isn't what the model can do. It's what can be shown about what
it did, on which date, with whose name against it.

Good luck with the climb. The evidence is going to matter more than the ambition, and you already sound like someone
who knows it.
