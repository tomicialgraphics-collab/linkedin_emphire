## The recommendation, for Behar to paste on his own profile

I was the independent technical verifier on his host failure benchmark. I ran the suite twice and raised issues on both passes. Where I disputed a verdict, it stayed disputed in the register rather than being quietly closed. He also insisted that the published claim say what the benchmark does not cover, other filesystems and concurrent writers among them, and put that limit where a reader would meet it rather than in a footnote.

If you are hiring him to break or verify something, expect to be told exactly what the result does not show. That is the part I would hire for.

## How to send it

Send the draft, not the instructions. One message, three lines:

"I wrote a draft of what I would say about the review. It is below and it is yours to change, cut or ignore. Only your name goes on it, so only your version counts."

Then set the relationship to **Other** and describe it as an independent technical review. "Worked together" would describe a job you did not have, and a recommendation that misstates the relationship is the first thing a careful reader discounts.
