POCs compare the two platforms on a good day. The migration gets decided on a bad one: a feed that half loads, a source that goes quiet for an hour, a column that changes type overnight. In regulatory reporting that is also where the platform's own audit trail earns its keep, because a green report means nothing if the check behind it never ran. I would add one line to your POC checklist: break something on purpose, then read what the platform says about it afterwards. That answer separates the two faster than any feature comparison.
