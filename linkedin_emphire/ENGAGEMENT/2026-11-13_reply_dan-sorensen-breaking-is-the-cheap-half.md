## Reply, paste as is

Breaking is the cheaper half, and I'd argue that gently. The expensive part is making the break repeatable. A finding
nobody can re-run turns into an anecdote inside a month, and then the fix quietly drifts back to the old state with
nobody watching.

That is also the argument for a team without a red teamer on staff. What they need is one test that runs on every
release and goes red by itself, built once from a break somebody else found. The person who writes that test is
usually the engineer who owns the system, not the person who broke it.

Which is a longer way of agreeing with you about skills, and saying the skill is narrower than it sounds.
