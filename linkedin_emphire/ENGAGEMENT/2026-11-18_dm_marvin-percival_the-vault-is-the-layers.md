An end-user application, and the vault underneath it is the foundation layer. What I have in mind is a property register with a document vault: the asset, its papers, and a fingerprint of every paper that anyone can check without asking me. If I build the foundation on its own, nobody ever touches it. If I build the vault, the foundation is the thing people are already using.

One thing I'd change in what you said. Lagos is crowded too. The difference isn't the competition, it's the distance, because here I can sit in the room when someone's survey plan comes back wrong and watch what they do next. From the US I'd be reading about it a week later.

Free for the first 10 users is fine. Just so we're clear with each other, that's a test, not the business.

And I want to argue with you about one rule, since you gave me permission. FORAY's rule is that the service never holds client data. A vault holds it. So either the vault is a different animal, or that rule needs a boundary drawn somewhere I can't see yet. I'd rather find that out from you than from a customer.

Send me your PRD template, or tell me to write page one, and I'll start tonight.
