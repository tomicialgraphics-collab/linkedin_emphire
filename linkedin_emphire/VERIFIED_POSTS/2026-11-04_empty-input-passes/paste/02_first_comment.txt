That's a one-line fix worth doing today: before the loop, assert the collection isn't empty and the count matches what you expected. A test that can pass over zero cases reports on nothing.

The question I'd ask in a review: what does your output look like when the input is missing? If it looks the same as a pass, the check is decorative.
