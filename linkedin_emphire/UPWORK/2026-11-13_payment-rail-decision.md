# Should the Upwork profile link go to Kuldeep and Favre

Short answer: not as the first move, and not at all until the profile says what the price says.

## What the profile currently is, on the record

`PLANS/2026-10-10_upwork-rebrand-empire.md` records the live state as of 9 October: headline "Timothy O. - Shopify &
TikTok Shop Strategist VA", rate $20/hr, 33% complete, five ecommerce portfolio items. The same file sets the intended
rebrand: "AI Red Teaming | LLM & RAG Security Testing | Prompt Injection" at $65/hr, with a new overview and catalogue.
Whether that rebrand was executed on the profile is not recorded here and cannot be checked from this sandbox, so it is
an open question for him to answer in ten seconds by opening his own profile.

That difference decides everything. A founder who is quoted $1,500 and clicks a $20/hr Shopify VA profile is being told
two different stories in one afternoon, and the cheaper one travels faster. At $20/hr, $1,500 reads as seventy five
hours of assistant work rather than one week of security engineering.

## What I would do for these two deals

Invoice directly. Kuldeep is first degree and asked for terms in writing; Favre invited the review himself and runs an
organisation that pays contractors by invoice. Neither needs escrow to be persuaded, both are technical, and both are
small enough that a fee of 10% is real money on a $1,500 job.

The terms to send: fixed fee, invoice on delivery, seven days, bank transfer or Wise. If either of them asks for
protection rather than offering it, that is the moment to offer escrow, not before.

## If escrow is wanted

Send an Upwork contract rather than a profile link: fixed price, one milestone, funded before the start date. Two rules
from the Upwork file apply and both are hard. The freelancer service fee is 10%, so $1,500 nets $1,350 and the number in
the contract should be $1,650 if the net is to hold. And Upwork's circumvention rule runs for two years after first
contact, so a deal started on the platform stays on the platform; asking for a bank transfer halfway through is the
fastest way to lose the account.

## The line to send instead

"Payment is an invoice on delivery with seven days to pay, by bank transfer or Wise. If you would rather have the work
held in escrow, say so and I will send it through Upwork instead."

It gives him the rail without volunteering a profile that may still be selling ecommerce work, and it keeps the choice
with the client, which is where it belongs on a first job.
