Page two, and all three corrections are right. I would rather take them now than build past them.

One. The fingerprint matches a file, not a paper, so the product stops pretending otherwise. The agency registers the file and the buyer is given that same file rather than a photocopy of it, which means the thing being checked is the thing that was stored. If someone turns up holding a paper copy, the vault says plainly that it cannot check that and points them at the registered file. That is a worse sentence for the product and a true one.

Two. The line about when it stopped being is cut. What the record can say is that this version was registered on a date, and that a different version was registered on another date. It can bracket a change between two dates. It cannot date the edit.

Three. Who registers, and my answer is an accountable party rather than an anonymous one. An agency or a lawyer with a verified account registers, and every registration carries their name and the date. A seller can still register a forged plan first, and then the record says who did it and when, which is the only thing that helps a buyer afterwards. When two versions exist, both are shown in order, each with the name of whoever registered it, and the page says that two versions exist. It never picks the one that is true, because it cannot know.

What I will store and where. The file, its fingerprint, who registered it, when, and who it was shown to. Not the buyer's identity, not their address, not their bank details. Files would be encrypted at rest and served through a link that expires. I would keep the documents in Nigeria if the cost is sane, and if they ever leave the country the transfer gets written down before it happens rather than after. When someone asks for deletion, the file goes and the fingerprint record stays, because the record is the point.

I have not checked any of this against the Act or the directive yet. Your links are the reading list, and I will start with the official text before the summaries, since you were right that four of them are articles. That reading comes before the first line of upload code.

Page three is the flow, if you want it. What a buyer does in the first five minutes, and what the record looks like when they show it to a lawyer a year later.
