**What convinces this ICP.** Not thought leadership. A reproducible artifact that
names a failure class present in their stack, with the inputs and the rate. They are buying
someone who can hand their auditor a document. Every article, every comment and every post
that does not end in a document is brand work, not pipeline.

**The funnel constraint nobody has priced in.** Of 100 ICP targets, the sheet found **5**
published email addresses. The other 95 are `PATTERN` (a guess against a confirmed domain) or
`DM LANE`. The sheet's own rule is never to cold-send a pattern address. So the email lane
has five usable rows in it, and the real lane is LinkedIn DM, which is rate-limited by

therefore not a nice-to-have for this ICP. It is the only top of funnel that exists, and the

## 6. The distribution diagnosis

Your comments get 5,808, 1,176, 346 and 363 impressions. Your own posts get 76, 26 and 27.

That is not a quality problem, it is an audience problem. A comment is displayed to an audience
that already exists and was already assembled by someone else. A post has to assemble its own
audience, and that requires a claim strong enough to be argued with, stated in the first two
lines. Your recent posts opened with setup, not a claim. The one you are posting today opens
with an accusation aimed at the reader, and it is the first post in this repo with a sourced
number from a third party.

Three consequences:
1. **Fewer posts, better armed.** One a week with a claim and a number beats five with context.
2. **Comments stay, but they must convert.** A comment that produces no DM is entertainment.
3. **The 1k follower gate has a date consequence.** Premium unlocks DM reach at 1k. At the
   current pace that is a Q1 2027 event, which means the DM lane stays expensive through the
   whole 70-day window. Do not plan revenue on it.
