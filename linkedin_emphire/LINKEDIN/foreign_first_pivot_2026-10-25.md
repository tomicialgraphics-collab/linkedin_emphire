## 0. What changed and why (the research basis)

The research already verified that the strongest FOREIGN dated
hooks are at least as strong as the local one:
- EU AI Act Article 50 is LIVE (applied 2 Aug 2026, not
  postponed); the machine-readable marking grace for
  pre-August generative systems CLOSES 2 Dec 2026 (~5 weeks
  out); interoperable watermark detection 2 Feb 2027;
  penalties to EUR 15M / 3% (primary EU source + Reg (EU)
  2026/1744).
- The AI-native tier (Segment B) is the tier literally hiring:
  45 open AI-security roles across 24 companies, median
  $192K-$270K; the most active employers are foreign (Vercel,
  Harvey, Brex, Twilio, Ramp). The interim window before the
  hire lands is the buying window.
- Mid-market (Segment A): 65% of $10B+ orgs have a head of AI
  security, but the role is dedicated at only 9% of $1B-$10B
  and 6% of under-$1B; IBM 2026: 54 agent incidents/company/
  year. They outsource red teaming.
- The existing content audience is already foreign: SF Bay 9%,
  DC 4%, NYC 4%, Seattle 3%, London 3%. The 4x vulnzero
  out-performer is speaking to a foreign demo.

So foreign-first is not a guess; it is pointing the offer at
where the verified dated triggers AND the existing impressions
already are. Nigeria remains the single strongest
"regulator-made the purchase necessary" finding (CBN circular,
mandated annual third-party audit, 2% guardrail coverage,
escalating fines) and the owner keeps the in-person trust
speed edge there. That is why it is DEMOTED to a secondary
trust lane, not deleted.

Honesty note: "more than local" is read as a comparative effort
shift (foreign primary, Nigeria secondary). If the owner meant
"only foreign," the Nigeria lane in this doc is dropped entirely
and the three local leads go cold; say the word and it is
removed.

## 2. What it means for POSTS (content engine)

The vulnzero teardown (the 4x out-performer) now LEADS with
named FOREIGN claims and incidents. Three tracks per week,
foreign-first:
1. FOREIGN agent-product / vendor claim teardown (named claim,
   the check, the rewrite) -> Tier 1. Ammunition (all verifiable,
   citable): EchoLeak (M365 Copilot), ForcedLeak / PipeLeak
   (Salesforce Agentforce), the CVSS 9.4 Claude Code + Gemini CLI
   + GitHub Copilot prompt-injection chain, CVE-2026-21520
   (Copilot Studio), 492 unauthenticated MCP servers, 1,184
   malicious ClawHub skills, the July 2026 Hugging Face / OpenAI
   breach.
2. EU AI ACT Article 50 mechanics (the dated hook) -> Tier 2.
   Teach it precisely: "Article 50 has applied since 2 August;
   the marking grace for pre-August generative systems closes 2
   December; penalties to EUR 15M." This is the dated-urgency
   content that converts Tier 2. Do NOT frame it as "pushed to
   2027."
3. Enterprise controls / questionnaire / mid-market agent
   incident -> Tier 3. E.g. Equifax's public containment model,
   the IBM 54-incidents stat, the "responsibility exists, the
   dedicated head does not" gap.

NIGERIA CONTENT: DEMOTED. No dedicated Nigeria content track
during the foreign-first sprint. A Nigeria story runs only if it
is a genuinely strong named claim with a number (e.g. a specific

are; Nigeria content does not move that demo toward a close.
