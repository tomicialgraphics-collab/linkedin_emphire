# Comment on Nadir Ali's situational leadership post, 15 November 2026

What this is: one comment that takes his three questions and shows what they turn into when the delegate is an
agent. What to do next: paste it once. No link, no pitch.

## Why this angle

His discipline is who decides, what gets delegated, when the leader steps in. That is authority design, which is
also what the owner does for a living, and the gap between the two is the comment: a person who oversteps can be
coached, and an agent that oversteps can only be bounded.

The examples are chosen from his own history rather than from AI. Refunds, payouts and merchant onboarding are
the nouns of a payments executive who built acquiring at Neoleap and ran operations at CASHU. Written that way,
the comment reads as a colleague thinking out loud rather than as a vendor introducing a category.

The closing paragraph keeps his own final point intact and makes it harder: the structure is deployable in a
sprint, the habit is not, and the agent keeps acting while the habit catches up.

## Comment, paste this

The harder version arrives when the delegation goes to something that can't grow into the role.

A new manager who oversteps can be coached, because he learns the context and changes. An agent given authority
over a refund, a payout, or the rule deciding which merchant gets onboarded doesn't learn what you meant. It
either has a boundary that can refuse, or it does whatever its instructions permit, and you find out in the
reconciliation a week later.

So the questions you name get harder rather than easier. Who decides becomes which component is allowed to say
no. What gets delegated becomes what the action is allowed to touch. When the leader steps in becomes what
happens by itself when the check fails. In most systems I test, those answers exist in a design document and
nowhere in the code.

Which is why your last point is the hard one. A structure can be deployed in a sprint. The habit of intervening at
the wrong level takes a year, and by then the agent has made the same judgement all week with nobody noticing it
sat outside the boundary.
