## Reply, paste as is

The comparisons fail on timing more than on character. Jobs came back once the argument that pushed him out had been
settled inside the company, and Altman came back in days because the people who could reverse it were the people who
wanted him back. Both of those were negotiations, and negotiations have deadlines.

A return here depends on a review, not a negotiation: whether anything reached Beijing. Nobody in this thread knows the
answer, the board may not either, and the 1,600 comments asking for him cannot move the thing that decides it. History
gives you the shape of a comeback. It does not give you the date of a report.
