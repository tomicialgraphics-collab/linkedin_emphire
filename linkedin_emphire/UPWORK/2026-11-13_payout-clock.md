# When the money actually lands

Four clocks run in sequence, and none of them start when the work is done. They start when a button is pressed.

## Fixed price, which is every catalog project and the Sudhir milestone

| Step | Who moves | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Client funds the milestone | client, before work starts | day 0 |
| You submit the work for payment | you | the clock starts here |
| Client approves | client | any time, up to 14 days |
| Client does nothing | nobody | auto-releases on day 14 |
| Five day security hold | Upwork | 5 days, after approval |
| Withdraw to a Nigerian bank | you | up to 4 business days |

Approved on the day you submit: **about 9 days** from submit to naira in the account.
Left to auto-release: **about 23 days**.

Nothing moves while the milestone sits funded and unsubmitted. A funded milestone with no submission is money in
somebody else's account, and the most common reason a first payment "never arrives" is that the work was delivered in
chat and never submitted through the contract.

## Hourly

The week runs Monday to Sunday. The client is billed the following Monday, and the funds become available the Wednesday
after that, about ten days after the week closed. Then withdrawal.

## Withdrawal to Nigeria, the part that catches people

| Method | Fee | Time | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct to Local Bank, naira | $0.99 | within 4 business days | $3,000 per withdrawal, $5 minimum |
| Payoneer | a small per transfer fee, then about 2 percent when Payoneer converts to naira | about 24 hours to Payoneer, then 1 to 3 days to the bank | set by Payoneer |

Two timing traps:

1. **Direct to Local Bank processes same day only if the request is in before 12:00 UTC, which is 1pm in Lagos.** After
   that it is the next business day before the four day count starts.
2. **A newly added withdrawal method cannot be used for three full days.** Adding a bank account on the morning the money
   becomes available means three more days of waiting. Add it now, while there is nothing to withdraw.

## The one thing to do today

If there is no withdrawal method on the account, add one. It costs nothing, it takes ten minutes, and it starts a three
day clock that otherwise starts on the day the money arrives.

Then check the Sudhir milestone: if it is funded and the work is delivered, the submission is owed. The five day hold
does not begin until the client approves, and the approval does not begin until the work is submitted.
