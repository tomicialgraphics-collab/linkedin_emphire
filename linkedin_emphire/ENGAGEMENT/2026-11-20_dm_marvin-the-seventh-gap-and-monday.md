## Message, paste as is

<!-- gate:body:start -->
Read your levels again after the call and found a seventh gap, and it's the one that makes the other six worth having.

Nothing in the document has the registering agency declare anything. REQ-010 records who created the file. REQ-024 lets a verifier attest under their Stele identity. But no requirement captures the agency saying, in its own name, what it is putting on the record and what it is holding. So the name under the level tells a lawyer who touched the file, not who stands behind it, and you're the one who said a name is something a lawyer can call.

What I'd add is one sentence at registration, chosen and stored by the agency, shown on the certificate under its name. We registered this file on this date, we hold a copy of what we registered, and we checked it against the document the buyer was given. Not a warranty of title, which nobody can honestly give. A statement of what the agency did and what it holds, which is the thing it can be asked about in a year.

It also does something for level three. Two versions, neither marked true, and the only thing that separates them is who registered when and what each of them said they held.

Monday, anchor test before anything else, as I said. Two answers I still need from you: is the NIPOST key live or a test key, and can Stele be mounted this week.

I'll send v0.4 as a redline over the weekend with all seven in it, so we're arguing with a document instead of a thread.
