## Rotation

- Never the same template two days in a row.
- Mix technical with personal and wisdom in any week.
- Every bracket gets a specific detail, never a placeholder.
- Every post ends on a statement. The template's closing question is optional and usually cut.

## Notes

- **T1 Technical deep-dive**: The default template for this lane. Fixes the question and ends on a statement.
- **T2 Personal story**: Only with a real event on a real date. No composite stories.
- **T3 Life coaching**: Off-lane. Use sparingly and never in a week with a client deliverable.
- **T4 Leadership lesson**: Works when the decision is technical and the lesson is organisational.
- **T5 Faith-inspired wisdom**: Never auto-selected for this lane.
- **T6 Observational insight**: Pairs well with a screenshot or a small real artefact.
- **T7 Educational framework**: The framework must be yours, not a paraphrase of someone else's.
- **T8 Book lesson**: Quote short, attribute exactly, never at length.
- **T9 Opinion piece**: Concede first. Never strawman.
- **T10 Fictional parable**: Must be labelled as a story, never passed off as a real event.
- **T11 Behind the scenes**: Best with a run log, a diff, or a screenshot from the actual work.
- **T12 Inspirational narrative**: Highest risk of hype. Gate on R06 before it ships.
- **T13 Volunteering and community**: Off-lane unless it is a conference or an open-source contribution.
- **T14 Conversation starter**: Violates R15 if the post is a bare question with no statement in it. Answer first, then ask.
