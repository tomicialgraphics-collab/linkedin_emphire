# Comment on Ricky Jones's round table post, 7 November 2026

## What this post is, and why the usual comment would be wrong

It is a thank-you note, a piece of myth-making, and an introduction of a mentor to an audience, in that order. It
contains no claim about agents, no design, and nothing to test.

Under this post a comment about authority boundaries would be a man walking into a wake with a slide deck. The
relationship is worth more than the remark, and the remark would cost the relationship. So the comment stays in his
register and is short.

## Draft, paste as a comment

Handing the sword back is the right ending, and it says more about how you run a room than the reveal does. A table
where nobody needs a title is harder to keep than one with a throne, because the only thing holding it together is
that people keep choosing to turn up.

Tell Merlina she found a good student.

## Why this shape

- Four sentences. Under a post this personal, length is the thing that marks you as an outsider.
- The one idea in it is the only serious one available without changing the subject: a table with no throne is held
  by consent rather than by authority, which is a real distinction and happens to be his professional subject, said
  obliquely enough that it reads as a compliment rather than a segue.
- The last line credits the person the post is about. That is what such a post is for, and it is what he will
  remember.
- No link, no pitch, no offer of a call, and no mention of agent authority. That conversation belongs under his
  technical posts, not here.
