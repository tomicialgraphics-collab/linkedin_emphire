What stops most teams is the step after adoption. Sixty-four percent of engineers using agents is a headcount figure, and it moves the constraint to whoever reads the output. Once power users run five sessions at a time, review becomes the queue, and the number worth quoting stops being how many pull requests the agent opened and becomes how many survived review without being rewritten. Most teams can produce the first figure on request and can't produce the second, because nothing records what happened to a change after it merged. Adoption is easy to count, which is why it gets quoted.
