# Borrowed audience board

R42. One appearance in front of twenty thousand people does what a month of posting cannot, and the
lead time is weeks. This board is the only place a borrowed audience ask exists. An ask that is not a
row here did not happen, and a follow-up that is not a date here will be forgotten.

## The cadence

- **Two asks a week.** Not five and not zero. The number is low because each one is a written pitch to
  a specific host about a specific episode, which is an hour's work when it is done properly.
- **A follow-up seven days after the ask**, as a date in the row, whether or not they replied.
- **No ask without a named route.** A route is how the person actually receives messages: a public
  email from their own site, a DM they answer, or an introduction from somebody who knows them. Where
  the route is not yet known, the row says so and the state is `prospect`, which is not an ask.
- **Nothing is sent by the agent.** The owner sends, every time (R28).

## The board

State is one of `prospect` (named, route unknown), `ready` (route known, pitch not written),
`pitched` (ask sent, waiting), `booked`, `declined`, `dormant`.

## Why these eight

Three practitioner security shows, two evals shows, one large product audience. The lane rule from the
growth plan still applies: five thousand practitioners in evaluation and security is worth more than
fifty thousand general readers. B1 to B4 are the lane. B5 to B8 are the audience that commissions the
work, and they are the ones who convert a follower into a client.

## The one thing this board cannot do

It cannot tell you whether a host will say yes, and it cannot make the pitch for you. What it does is
make the second week visible: with two asks a week, the seventh ask goes out in week four, which is
still inside the twenty five days that remain before the target date stops being reachable.
