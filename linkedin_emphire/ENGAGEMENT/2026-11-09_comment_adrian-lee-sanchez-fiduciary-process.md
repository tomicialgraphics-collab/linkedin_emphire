attaches to process: a board that has identified the dependency, documented the succession bench and tested it can defend a flat

## Comment, paste as is

The market cap isn't the threshold. Fiduciary duty attaches to process rather than price. A board that has identified the dependency, written the bench down and tested it can defend a flat structure at almost any valuation. A board that hasn't is exposed at a fraction of this size.

What this week adds is that the exposure isn't only succession. The reported targeting ran through a relationship, and no access review covers who an executive trusts. Directors audit systems. Almost nobody audits discretion.

Two hundred billion is a rough price on how much of the strategy lives in one person's judgement rather than in a document.
