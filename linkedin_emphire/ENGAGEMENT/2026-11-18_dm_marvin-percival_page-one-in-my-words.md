Page one, in my words, and short as you asked. You were right about free. The first users will pay something small, because free tells me nothing about whether they would pay, and small still tells me.

Who uses it. Someone in Lagos about to hand over a year's rent or a cut of the sale price, holding a photocopy of a survey plan and a deed, unable to tell whether the paper in their hand is the one the seller showed somebody else, or the one the next buyer will be shown. Behind them, the small agency or the lawyer who has to say what was handed over and when.

What problem. Not whether a title is good. I can't tell anyone that, and I won't pretend to. The narrow problem is the copy. A paper gets edited, replaced or denied, and there is no record of what was shown, to whom, on what day. The vault holds the paper and the date. FORAY holds the fingerprint. Later, anyone holding the same paper can check it is the same paper that was registered then, and if it isn't, when it stopped being.

What I won't build. I won't say a property is safe to buy. No title search, no ownership check, no verified badge. A forged survey plan takes a fingerprint as well as a real one, and if the product ever hints otherwise, somebody loses money on my word. I won't hold money, broker, or run an escrow. No listings, no marketplace, no agent directory.

One thing I owe you before a real user uploads anything. The vault makes me responsible for other people's land papers, so the data protection question comes before the build, not after it. I have not looked at it yet and I am not going to guess at it in a page.

Tell me what is wrong with it and I will fix it before page two.
