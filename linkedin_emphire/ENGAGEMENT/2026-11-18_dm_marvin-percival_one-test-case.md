Marvin, here is the one test case. Short version, with a page attached.

Sponsors pay for trial data they cannot independently check. The site holds the source documents, the sponsor holds a transcription, and the only bridge between them is a monitoring visit months later. So the question a dispute always lands on is what the site's record looked like at the time, and nobody outside the site can answer it.

What I want to build is one evidence record per participant event, written when the event happens, with the document hashed and anchored through FORAY. The document never leaves the site. Someone holding the bundle can then check that the record existed by a date and has not changed, without trusting the site or us.

Two of your pieces do the work: FORAY for the evidence, Stele for the signature, so an event names a person rather than an inbox.

One question I can't answer, and it decides who the buyer is: in a regulated trial, is anchoring evidence a legal question or an operational one? If it's operational, the buyer is a quality director. If it's legal, it's a general counsel, and the product changes shape.

The page has the three modules, what I'm deliberately leaving out, and how I'd test it in four weeks with a stranger and a browser.
