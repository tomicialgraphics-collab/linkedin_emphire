# The four links, read once, and why not five times a day

## The request, answered plainly

**I cannot run anything daily.** Every message starts a fresh session with no scheduler and no memory of the previous session's run. Anything I promise to do "each day" while you are away is a promise that quietly does not happen.

**Clicking them five times a day would also damage the only number that currently tells you anything.** Views are the metric we are trying to make meaningful. Twenty loads a day from one machine converts a measurement into a self-portrait: the 23 catalogue views become 23 of yours, and any rise later cannot be told apart from your own clicking. Upwork also counts a repeat visit by the same visitor once, so five clicks is one view or none. Nothing about that gets you an order.

**What does work: ask me to check when you write, and I diff against a baseline.** That is recorded below, with the four links in their canonical form, so the next check costs one read and produces a list of what actually changed.

And the thing that genuinely changes daily is on one page you can read in ten seconds: the services page, where the view and order counts sit beside each listing.

## What the crawl found that matters

**All four pages load logged out, so every listing is live.** That settles the toggle question from 11:56 WAT: visible, not hidden.

**The character counts are public.** The booking page renders your three requirements under "Here's what Timothy will need to know before you meet", and each one ends with its own length in brackets: "(101 characters)", "(142 characters)", "(111 characters)". This is no longer a suspicion from the editor screenshot. A client sees it today.

**The consultation already uses all five expertise chips:** Chatbot Development, Cybersecurity & Data Protection, User Testing, full-stack development, ai app development. So it is not three used and two free, it is five used and two to swap. User Testing and full-stack development are the two that pull the wrong buyer.

**His own page put him last on price and proof.** The "People like you also viewed" rail under the consultation lists four Top Rated Plus consultants at $35, $50, $50 and $200 with 1,283, 3,655, 2,629 and 1,787 reviews. At $40 with no reviews he is the cheapest and least proven card on his own page. That is the argument for the consultation being an entry point rather than an earner.

**The two projects are filed better than the consultation.** The red team listing sits under "Other Cybersecurity & Data Protection" and the chatbot listing under "Chatbots" in AI and Machine Learning. Only the consultation sits in the generic Development and IT leaf. The category fix is a one-listing job, not a three-listing one.

**The red team listing's comparison table is half empty.** Tiers run $150, $600 and $1,500 over 3, 7 and 14 days, with fast delivery at $75. All three company-size rows render blank, so a buyer reads a table with three empty cells. Tick the sizes you will take.

**The chatbot listing carries a $1 add-on.** "14-day re-test guarantee (+ 1 Day)" is priced at +$1 beside "extra integration +$150". A dollar add-on reads as a typo and cheapens a $2,500 top tier. Either fold the re-test into Standard and Advanced or price it like the work it is.

**Two things I could not read and will not guess at.** Both listings and the consultation show their FAQ questions but no answers in the public text, so the answers may be collapsed behind an accordion or may not be published. Check them from your own view. And the profile page carries a chip reading "Features 33% Complete" beside a banner about a new way to navigate profiles; I do not know what that percentage measures, and it may well not be profile completeness, but if the dashboard shows a completeness figure anywhere near it, that figure gates badge eligibility and is worth the hour it takes to close.

**The public profile's work history section renders "No items".** The page also says there are more jobs behind a sign-up wall, so this is partly a logged-out limit. It is still consistent with the 12-month earnings reading of $0 and worth the check on transaction dates.
