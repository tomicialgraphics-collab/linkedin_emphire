# 30 people to follow from the follower list of 6 November 2026

Owner request: from the 1,017-follower list, the 30 best ICP to follow on 6 November, with URLs.

## What ICP means here, stated before the list

The person who owns or builds the point where an AI system's decision has to be defensible, and who can
spend, refer, hire, cite or amplify. In practice that is runtime governance, execution boundaries, audit
evidence, and agent security. Second tier is assurance and evaluation, because that is where evidence
requirements get written into contracts. This is the same reader the FORAY register and the F9b work are
written for.

## How the URLs were checked, and one limit to state plainly

no profile could be opened here to confirm it by eye. A URL is given only where something outside LinkedIn
ties the slug to the person: the project's own records from a previous profile read, the person's own site,
or the person's own GitHub attributing their account. Everything else carries a name-exact search link, which
is a search and not a claim about a profile. No slug has been invented, and none should be trusted as
click-verified.

a compliment and not a pitch. The four with a real URL are the cheapest, because the profile opens straight
away. Then two comments, on the two posts nearest your argument from the last seven days. A follow with no comment is a
number; a comment on a decision-boundary post is a conversation.
