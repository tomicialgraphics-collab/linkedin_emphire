# Sources and verification

Checked on 2026-11-12, the day of the announcement. Every factual line in the post is traceable to one of these.

## Primary

**NVIDIA newsroom, "NVIDIA Announces CEO Transition", 12 November 2026.** Read in full at
`nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition`, reached through the link the company's own
announcement circulated. It supplies four things the post uses and nothing else:

- Huang resigned as President, CEO and board member, effective immediately.
- Kress "will continue to serve as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer while serving as
  Interim Chief Executive Officer until a permanent successor has been named". The post paraphrases this and
  attributes the arrangement to the announcement.
- Harvey C. Jones appointed "its first-ever Chair of the Board", described as a former chief executive and
  executive chairman of Synopsys.
- The board "will consider internal and external candidates".

The release does not state a reason for the resignation and does not mention the investigation. That silence
is noted in the earlier comment file on Eva Benn's post and is not used in this post.

## Share price at the open

Reuters, carried by Times of India and India Today on 12 November, reports shares down 13% at the open
(13.09% at $150.98 in the Times of India market report). Quartz reports about 8% shortly after the bell, AP
about 9.7%, CoinDesk a fall of as much as 14% intraday. The spread is why the post says "opened 13% down" and
nothing else. No figure for lost market value is used, because the reporting varies between roughly 700 billion
and the whole-of-week total.

## Sequence, for context not used in the post body

New York Times first report 29 October; Huang admits an affair 8 November and the board opens an independent
investigation; second relationship reported 11 November with an emergency board meeting; resignation 12
November; Congress hearing set for 18 November. Sources: BBC live coverage timeline and PC Gamer's timeline.
Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz appointed for the investigation, per the Guardian.

## Claims and their basis

| Claim in the post | Basis |
| --- | --- |
| The interim chief executive is also still the chief financial officer | the company's own release, quoted |
| Those two jobs are separate because one attests to what the other runs | standard separation-of-duties principle, stated as a principle and not attributed to Nvidia's policy |
| The board had no chair before this week | the release calls Jones the first-ever chair |
| The failover ran for the first time in 33 years | the release refers to 33 years; Huang co-founded the company in 1993 |
| Exceptions granted in an emergency rarely carry an end date | a general claim about practice, not a finding about this company |

## What is not claimed

- No allegation about the relationships is repeated. The post says nothing about conduct, and the word is not in
  the body.
- No claim that Nvidia has failed to write a compensating control. The post does not have the internal record and
  says the risk is in what is not documented, which is a prediction about practice rather than an accusation.
- No claim that the arrangement is unlawful or improper. It is a governance trade-off made under time pressure,
  and the post says the independent chair is a real check on it.
- No figure for market value lost, no figure for revenue at risk, nothing about the investigation's findings.
