# Comment on Taha Hussain's emotional illiteracy post, 6 November 2026

## Draft, paste as a comment, 144 words

The list is right, and the thing I would add is that engineers are not short on emotion. They are short
on a container for it.

"Disagree without drama" stays a virtue until it gets a format. The format that works is dull: one
page, the two options, the constraint that rules one out, what each costs if it is wrong, and who owns
the call, with a date. Six paragraphs in Slack is what happens when there is no page.

Same with the feedback one. Most spiralling is answering an inference when somebody handed you an
observation. Ask which one it is before you answer it, and half the spiral never starts.

Unavoidable is the right word. The person who writes the decision page becomes the person decisions
route through, and none of it depends on being the nicest engineer in the room.
