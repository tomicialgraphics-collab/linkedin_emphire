## Comment, paste as is

The turn is the wrong unit for the thing you are moving into.

A conversation eval can score every turn as helpful and still pass a system that said the right words and then booked the wrong slot, because the action landed after the turn closed. The unit that survives is the trajectory: what the user asked, what the system said, what it did, and what the record shows afterwards.

Design work is better preparation for this than it looks. You already notice the moment a persona breaks script, which is the same skill as spotting the case nobody wrote down. Evals are thin exactly there.

The question I would put to the room is what a trace has to contain before somebody who was not there can score it.
