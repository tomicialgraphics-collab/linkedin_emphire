## Comment, paste as is

Day four is the one that will be hardest to do honestly, and the one I would read first.

Preparedness surveys measure intent. Everyone says they are ready and almost nobody can name the last time their AI system failed, what it actually did, and what changed afterwards. Those three answers separate an organisation that has run something from one that has attended a briefing about it.

The gap is rarely skills. It is that no name is attached to the failure mode. When the answer to "who owns this when the model does the wrong thing" is a department, the gap survives the meeting.
