The objection this usually gets is that a red team without a single verdict is a red team that never blocks.

It still blocks. Consensual harm is a bigger set than people assume, and it gates exactly as it did before. What changes is the contested half, which stops pretending the verdict came from nowhere.

One practice that costs nothing: put the rubric's author on the rubric. A name, a date, and the position they held while scoring. Every contested pass then becomes a decision somebody made, and the next reader can disagree with a person instead of with a document.

A rubric with no author is not neutral. It is unattributed.
