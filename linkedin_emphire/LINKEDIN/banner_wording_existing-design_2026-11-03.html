Wording for the existing banner: same design, new words

Owner constraint: keep the layout, the concept, the photo, the speech bubble, the white bar, the colours. Keep "Software Engineer". Change only the words, and only enough to make the biggest text on the profile say something specific.

The problem is one word

The banner reads "Software Engineer Building Solutions That Actually Work in Production". Everything in it is fine except the biggest word on the page, which is "Solutions", the most generic noun in the industry. It costs nothing to keep it and it earns nothing. Swap that one word and the whole banner moves without touching a single shape.

Grammar constraint worth knowing: the bar says "That Actually Work", which is plural, so the big word has to be plural or the bar has to change with it. Keep that in mind with any substitution.

Rules for this banner

  • Straight apostrophe in "Let's". The current banner uses a curly one, which is a small tell.
  • No em dashes anywhere.
  • Do not add a URL or an email. Both are already in Featured and About.
  • Do not add numbers. A banner cannot carry the evidence for them.
