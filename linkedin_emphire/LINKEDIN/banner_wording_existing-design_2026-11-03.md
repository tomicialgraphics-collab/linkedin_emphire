# Wording for the existing banner: same design, new words

Owner constraint: keep the layout, the concept, the photo, the speech bubble, the white bar, the colours.
Keep "Software Engineer". Change only the words, and only enough to make the biggest text on the profile
say something specific.

## The problem is one word

The banner reads "Software Engineer Building **Solutions** That Actually Work in Production". Everything
in it is fine except the biggest word on the page, which is "Solutions", the most generic noun in the
industry. It costs nothing to keep it and it earns nothing. Swap that one word and the whole banner moves
without touching a single shape.

Grammar constraint worth knowing: the bar says "That Actually Work", which is plural, so the big word has
to be plural or the bar has to change with it. Keep that in mind with any substitution.

## Set A. Recommended.

| Slot | Current | New | Chars |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speech bubble | Let's build together | `Let's break it first` | 20 |
| Small line | Software Engineer Building | `Software Engineer Building` | unchanged |
| Big word | Solutions | `Defenses` | 8 vs 9 |
| White bar | That Actually Work in Production | `That Actually Work in Production` | unchanged |
| Footer | Web · AI · Computer Vision · Real Projects | `AI Security · Red Teaming · Agents · Evals` | 42 |

"Defenses" is one character shorter than "Solutions" and much wider per character in most condensed
faces, so the big line will sit at very close to its current width. Nothing else moves.

Why it works: "Defenses that actually work in production" is a claim a buyer can test, and it is exactly
what he does. The bubble stops being a sales line and becomes a stance.

## Set B. The smallest possible change.

| Slot | New | Chars |
|---|---|---|

| Small line | `Software Engineer Building` | keep |
| Big word | `Agents` | 6 vs 9 |

One word changes. "Agents" is short, so the big line will either leave a gap at the right or need eight to
ten percent more point size. If the design breaks, use Set A.

## Set C. Security-forward, two slots change.

| Slot | New | Chars |
|---|---|---|

The bar gets shorter, so the white pill will be narrower and the line will sit differently. This is the
only set that visibly moves a shape, so take it only if the designer can nudge the pill width.

## Big word substitutions, by width

Keep the width of the block roughly where it is and the layout does not need to change.

- 9 to 10 characters, current width: `Guardrails`, `Pipelines`
- 8 characters, near current width: `Defenses`, `Findings`, `Failures`
- 6 to 7 characters, will leave a gap: `Agents`, `Checks`, `Systems`, `Evals`
- Singular words need a new bar as well: `Assurance That Holds When It Counts`, `Evidence That Holds Up`

## Footer line options, at similar length to the current one

- `AI Security · Red Teaming · Agents · Evals` (42, recommended)
- `AI · Security · Evals · Real Projects` (37, keeps the breadth)
- `Web · AI · Agents · Security` (27, keeps the original rhythm and adds the lane)
- `Red Teaming · Agents · Evals · Tool Permissions` (47, most specific, longest)

Drop "Computer Vision" unless he is still selling that work. Every word on a banner competes with the
headline for the same three seconds.

## Bubble options

- `Let's break it first` (19)
- `Test it before launch` (21)
- `Break it before launch` (22)
- Keep `Let's build together` if the design depends on the tail of the bubble text

## Rules for this banner

- Straight apostrophe in "Let's". The current banner uses a curly one, which is a small tell.
- No em dashes anywhere.
- Do not add a URL or an email. Both are already in Featured and About.
- Do not add numbers. A banner cannot carry the evidence for them.
- Do not put the phrase "AI-powered" in it.
