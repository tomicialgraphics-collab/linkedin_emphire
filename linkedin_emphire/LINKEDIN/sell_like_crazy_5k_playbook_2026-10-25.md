# Sell Like Crazy -> the $5K playbook (2026-10-25)

Book: Sell Like Crazy, Sabri Suby (repo: books/sell-like-crazy.md,
6,066 lines). Prior extract: linkedin_emphire/STRATEGY/
BOOK_EXTRACTS/04_sell-like-crazy_principles.md (S1-S25, mapped
to the two Upwork listings + LinkedIn). This doc takes the
framework to the $5K goal and makes it executable.
Research + citations: LINKEDIN/first_5k_research_2026-10-25.md.

## 0. The one-line diagnosis

The book's Phase 1 is the whole problem: the owner has been
running Phase 5-6 (traffic: content, comments, followers)
without a finished Phase 4 (a Godfather Offer) and a defined
Phase 1 buyer. 753 followers, flat impressions, zero closes is
exactly what the book predicts: traffic with no offer converts
to audience, not revenue. The fix is not more traffic. The fix
is the offer + the buyer + the first-10-conversations system.

## 1. Phase 1 - Dream buyer (done in the research doc)

Two personas profiled with the nine questions (research doc
section 4): the founder mid-deal (Tier 2) and the Nigerian
bank security lead (Tier 1). Buyer vocabulary is extracted:
"the agent", "the evals", "the questionnaire", "proof",
"board assurance", "CBN requirement". All outbound copy uses
these words, not consultant words.
Rule S3 (Collier): enter the conversation already taking
place in the buyer's mind. The founder's conversation is "my
deal is stuck on security". The bank lead's conversation is
"the CBN wants proof our AI is controlled".

## 2. Phase 4 - The Godfather Offer (build this first)

S12: sell what the market wants. The market (verified) wants
an AI agent check between $5K and $8K that a buyer can get in
two weeks, because the standard market offer is $8K-$25K in
one to two months, and the tooling floor ($450-$2,500) is not
trusted for the agent-specific chain. The offer:

NAME: The Agent Gate (working name, owner to rename).
WHAT: one AI agent or AI chatbot, five tests (the five-tests
repo), two weeks, a re-runnable report.
PRICE: $5,000. 50% to book, 50% on report delivery.
ANCHOR (say it out loud, it is verified): "The market rate
for a one-time AI system audit is $8K-$25K; for a single
scoped agent the tooling floor is $450-$2,500 but nobody
trusts it for the agent-specific chain. This is the scoped
human version at $5K."
BENEFIT (Detail Sheet, S14, features to benefits):
- Five tests, executed and logged -> "a result with a method
  underneath it, not a conclusion"
- Re-runnable report (fingerprint: model, seed, temperature,
  prompt version, date) -> "anyone on your team, or the
  other side of the questionnaire, can re-run it and
  disagree using my setup"
- Severity + root-cause class + fix pattern per finding ->
  "the engineering team can start Monday, not after a
  workshop"
- The report is forwardable -> "it is the asset you send to
  procurement / the board / the auditor"
- 48-hour first finding (the Quick Scan, see 3) -> "you see
  the shape of the work before you trust me with the rest"
RISK REVERSAL: the 48-hour first finding is free in the price
and delivered before any deep work; if it finds nothing
material, the owner can walk with the scan in hand (this is
the honest version of "outrageous then pull back", S15).
URGENCY (only where a real date exists, verified):
- EU-exposed targets: "Article 50 has applied since 2 August;
  the marking grace period closes 2 December."
- Nigeria targets: "the CBN AML-AI roadmaps are in; the audit
  requirement is annual; your deadline is the CBN's, not
  mine."
- Startup targets: "your questionnaire has a due date; this
  finishes before it."
WHO ACTS NOW: the buyer with a dated trigger (deal, launch,
DD, regulator). Everyone else is the 97% (S6/S7): they get
the education content and the list, not the slot.
SLOT LIMIT: two $5K slots per month (scarcity must be real;
it is, delivery-wise).

## 3. Phase 2 - The HVCO (what we give away)

S8-S10: free, substantive, must make the prospect think
"imagine the paid version". The owner already has three
candidates, ranked:
1. THE CLAIM TEARDOWN (flagship). Public, on the record, one
  specific AI security or AI product claim, re-run and
  reported. The Truefolio review IS this format being built
  live right now: public half (numbers re-computed, the TWR
  without a denominator), in-app half, findings, asks. With
  Hod's OK, the published version is the single best HVCO
  asset the owner has, because it demonstrates the exact
  method on a named, real, frozen target. Next candidates:
  any public vendor claim with a number (the 10-24 post was
  this, generalized; a named one is stronger).
2. THE FIVE TESTS REPO (already exists, github.com/
  Teamthy/ai-redteam-five-tests). The buyer can run it on
  their own agent in-house. This is the "portfolio piece"
  the first-client research says you need
  (research doc section 3).
3. THE 48-HOUR QUICK SCAN. One specific finding in the
  buyer's agent or chatbot, free, in the price of the
  engagement. The 10-24 extract flagged this: it must be a
  concrete defect (one real issue, root cause, fix pattern),
  never a thin "free audit" that is a sales call in a coat.
HVCO distribution: the teardown = public post (LinkedIn, the
vulnzero format that out-earned the lens post 4x); the repo =
the DM asset ("here is the method, run it, tell me what
happens"); the Quick Scan = the diagnosis tool on the call.
OPEN DECISION (carried from the 10-24 extract): build an owned
email capture for the HVCO, or stay LinkedIn + DM only until
the ad gate. Recommended minimal version: a one-page site
(one-hour build) with the five-tests repo + the teardown + a
waitlist. Flagged, not built.

## 4. Phase 5-6 - Traffic, adapted (no paid ads, no funnel
tools)

The book's Magic Lantern (traffic -> opt-in -> HVCO ->
offer -> nurture) cannot run as written: Upwork bans
off-platform capture, LinkedIn has no opt-in page, the owner
has no list yet. The adaptation, using channels that ARE
allowed:
- LANTERN 1 (LinkedIn, the owned-ish one): 3 posts/week in
  the vulnzero format (named claim -> the check -> the
  rewrite). End-of-post CTA rotation: (a) none (pure
  education), (b) "the method is in the five-tests repo"
  (repo link in first comment), (c) once every two weeks,
  "two slots a month for the Agent Gate; the DM is open".
  The 10-25 analytics say the specific format is the 4x
  out-performer; the lens posts continue as connective tissue,
  not as the lead.
- LANTERN 2 (connection notes, the cold lane): 3-5/day from
  the 12 named prospects + the hiring-trigger companies,
  using the per-prospect approaches already specified in the
  client file. Note structure (verified by the channel
  research): specific observation about their context + one
  line on what I do for companies like theirs + one shared
  artifact + 15-minute ask. No pitch in message one.
- LANTERN 3 (DM nurture, the 97%): a three-step value sequence
  within LinkedIn DM limits: step 1 value (the teardown or a
  specific observation on their product), step 2 value (the
  five-tests repo, "run it, tell me what breaks"), step 3
  soft CTA (the 30-minute diagnosis, "no deck, I ask two
  questions and you decide"). No step is a hard sell (S16).
- LANTERN 4 (Upwork, the floor lane): the two existing
  listings retitled to the offer language (Agent Gate entry
  tier + the vCISO-bench tier); proposals only where the job
  has a dated trigger or a real scope; proposals open with
  the diagnostic question, not the credentials.
- LANTERN 5 (Nigeria, the trust lane): written-first outreach
  to the three named security leads, formal, CBN-circular-
  anchored, in-person offer. One per week, not all at once.
- LANTERN 6 (channel partners, the leverage lane): 1-2
  conversations with vCISO/fractional/MSP operators: "I am
  your AI red-team bench; you carry the board, I carry the
  evals and the report." Deal flow, not end clients.

## 5. Phase 7 - Sales conversion (the call)

S19: "a prescription without a diagnosis is malpractice."
The 30-45 minute free call, no deck:
- TWO DIAGNOSTIC QUESTIONS (asked before any price):
  1. "What does the agent touch: data, money, or external
     actions?"
  2. "What forces a check now: a deal, a launch, a
     regulator, or an incident?"
  (If neither answer exists, the buyer is the 97%; they get
  the content path, politely. S19: decline poor fits.)
- THEN the offer: the Anchor (verified market numbers), the
  five tests, the 48-hour first finding, the re-runnable
  report, the price, the 50/50, the two slots.
- ONE SPECIFIC FROM THEIR CONTEXT (the note's observation,
  applied live) - this is what separates the call from a
  brochure.
CLOSE METRIC: not "calls made" but "diagnostic questions
asked before price, per call" (the 10-24 extract's
measurement, kept).

## 6. The 30-day plan (numbers and kill criteria)

WEEK 1 (build the machine):
- D1-D2: Agent Gate offer one-pager (PDF, one page): the
  offer, the anchor, the five tests, the 48-hour finding, the
  price, the slots. (The Arcezia scope PDF is the template.)
- D2-D3: warm-network blast (TIER 0): the 5+ existing
  clients + the 30-name warm list (research doc section 3,
  customcareer source): the specific ask, the referral
  question, no generic "I'm available".
- D3-D4: Upwork listings retitled to the offer language.
- D4-D5: the Quick Scan spec written (what the 48-hour
  finding contains, what it does not).
- D5-D7: first 10 connection notes (named prospects +
  hiring triggers).

WEEK 2 (cold + trust lanes running):
- 3-5 notes/day (total ~15). First Nigeria written-first
  outreach (one lead). EU-deadline note to any EU-exposed
  target.
- First DM nurture sequences started (3 steps, spaced).
- 1-2 calls booked; diagnosis-first script in use.

WEEK 3 (proof + second wave):
- Publish the flagship teardown (Truefolio with Hod's OK, or
  the next named public claim) in the vulnzero format.
- Notes round 2 (fresh triggers, no repeat).
- 1-2 more calls. First 48-hour Quick Scan delivered (to
  whoever took the call).

WEEK 4 (close):
- Proposals out: target 2-3 at $5K (or 2 x $2.5K split).
- The first close is the goal; the second slot is the bonus.
- Retainer upgrade offered to the close (Path 5).

TARGETS (30 days, logged in the tracker):
- 40-50 connection notes; 8-12 accepts (15-25% warm rate is
  the cross-source benchmark - measure the actual).
- 6-10 DM conversations reaching step 2.
- 4-6 calls; 100% of calls with the diagnostic questions
  asked before price.
- 2-3 proposals; 1-2 closes = $5K-$10K.
KILL / PIVOT RULES (SLC discipline):
- Any channel under 5% reply after 20 touches: kill it, move
  the effort to the best channel.
- Zero calls by day 21: the offer is the problem, not the
  channel - re-price or re-scope (e.g. split to 2 x $2.5K,
  or the Quick Scan as a $990 entry product), then restart
  week 3.
- A close on any path: pause the cold lanes for the delivery
  week; the case study (anonymized per the redaction standard)
  becomes the next HVCO.

## 7. Measurement (from the 10-24 extract, section 3, kept)

Per channel, weekly, in the tracker:
- Notes: sent, accepts, reply rate.
- DMs: conversations started, reached step 2, reached step 3.
- Calls: booked, diagnostic asked before price (yes/no),
  proposal rate.
- Proposals: sent, close rate, average value.
- Upwork: proposals, replies, interviews, hires, per listing.
- Content: the vulnzero-format posts' impressions vs the lens
  posts (the 4x spread is the internal A/B test).
Follower demographics: watched, not chased (research doc
section 1, the analytics diagnosis).

## 8. What the book settles and what it does not

SETTLES: the order of operations (buyer -> offer -> HVCO ->
traffic -> conversion); the offer design discipline (Detail
Sheet, risk reversal, real scarcity, dated urgency only);
the call structure (diagnosis first, decline poor fits); the
nurture logic (the 97% is the pool, value-only steps);
measurement per channel.
DOES NOT SETTLE (owner decisions):
1. The offer name (Agent Gate is a working name).
2. $5,000 flat vs 2 x $2,5K split as the default quote.
3. The owned-list / one-page-site question (carried from
  10-24).
4. Whether the Truefolio teardown publishes, and in what
  shape (his OK is the gate, already agreed in principle).
5. The Nigeria lead who gets the first written-first note.
