# $5,000 by 25 December

Corrected goal: not followers. Five thousand dollars, received or agreed before Christmas, in 43 days.

## The starting line, from the log

`./emphire revenue` reads `STATE/REVENUE-LOG.md` and prints this without an opinion attached:

```
paid 90   agreed 0   proposed 0   of 5,000
days left 43   gap 4,910   gap per day 114
open conversations: 0
```

Ninety dollars, from one tutoring booking. Two engagements delivered and recorded at zero, one of them six
weeks long and the best work in the record. Nothing has ever been priced and put in front of anybody.

So this is not a conversion problem and not a reach problem. It is a first-sale problem, and the plan below is
built around that rather than around audience growth, which is a separate goal with a separate plan.

## Three shapes that add to 5,000

| Shape | Deals | Why it works or does not |
|---|---|---|
| A | one at 3,500 and one at 1,500 | The two offers already in `STRATEGY/2026-10-02_90-day-10k-client-positioning.md`, unchanged. Fewest conversations. |
| B | two at 2,500 | A price that exists nowhere in the record yet, so both conversations start by justifying a number nobody has quoted. |
| C | five at 1,000 | Most conversations, fastest close. A one-week fixed-scope review at 1,000 is a spend a team lead can approve without a procurement cycle. |

Take C as the volume engine and A as the upside. Five small closes is a realistic 43-day outcome from a
standing start. One 3,500 engagement is not something to plan on and is the thing to take if it appears.

## Where the money actually is, ranked by probability per hour spent

**1. The two people who already know the work.** Highest probability by a wide margin, and the least used.
Both have had weeks of unpaid attention and neither has been asked for a price.

- **Praveen, Arcezia.** Seven findings, all open, deliberately: a finding closes when a fix is verified. That is
  the paid next phase, and it is his own interest rather than ours. The fee waiver covers the review that was
  delivered; verifying fixes is a new scope and must be quoted as one. The constraints he set in writing hold:
  no load testing, no reverse engineering, and the write-up stays unpublished until he signs it off.
- **Marvin, FORAY.** Not yet. He is mid-read on the case study, and asking for money before he replies would
  make the case study look like a sales document. The order is: he reads, he replies, then a specific paid
  proposal for a scoped re-run of the probes he has been shown. One paragraph, one price, one week.

**2. Fixed-scope work on platforms.** The catalogue, the sample findings report and the proposal templates are
already in `UPWORK/`. The failure mode here is volume without a fixed scope, so every proposal names one
deliverable, one week, one price, and the sample report as the proof. Ten proposals a week is the quota; the
close rate on fixed-scope security reviews is low enough that fewer than ten is a hobby.

**3. Lagos and West African teams shipping AI into production.** Nobody in the record has worked this, and it
is the one place where being in Lagos is an advantage rather than something to explain. A 1,500 pre-launch
review, quoted in naira equivalent, sold to a fintech or health team that has an AI feature and no security
reviewer, is the single fastest thing on this list that is not a warm contact. Ten named teams, ten warm
introductions through the network, no cold email.

**4. Cold outbound at volume.** Fifth in order of return, and the only one that scales without permission.
`EMAIL/leads/2026-10-27_icp100.md` already holds the list.

Twenty-eight days of proposals before the first close is not pessimism, it is the observed lag between a
first conversation and a signature in work like this. If nothing is agreed by 29 November, the shape changes
from C-plus-A to C alone and the target becomes 3,000, stated on the day rather than discovered on the 24th.

## The things that would guarantee missing it

- **A third free engagement.** Two are already in the record and both were right. The third is the one that
  turns a portfolio into a habit, and it costs six weeks of the only currency that matters here.
- **Discounting the 1,500 review to 500 to win the first one.** The first paid deal sets the price the market
  believes, and a 500 review buys a 500 client who refers 500 clients.
- **Waiting for the case study to be public before selling anything.** Marvin's read has no date on it. The
  selling should not wait for a document that belongs to him.
- **Scope creep into development.** The positioning document of 2 October already forbids it and it is the
  fastest way to spend December building somebody's feature for 150 an hour.
- **Counting agreed money as paid money.** The log separates them for a reason; `won` is a promise and only
  `paid` buys the month.
