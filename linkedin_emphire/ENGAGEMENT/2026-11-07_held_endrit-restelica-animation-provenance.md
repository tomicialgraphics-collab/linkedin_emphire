## The draft, if the reach is worth more to you than the fit

The quality argument settles faster than people think. What keeps generated animation out of a studio pipeline is
not whether it looks like Pixar. It is whether anyone can say where each frame came from.

A generated shot has no chain: no plate, no asset list, no record of what the model was trained on or whose likeness
it borrowed. Studios ship against contracts that require that chain, and a deliverable nobody can trace is a legal
exposure before it is a creative one.

Taste gets you the first watch. Provenance gets you the release.
