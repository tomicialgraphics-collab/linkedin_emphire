## The angle

She asks whether people would trust an agent inside their bank. That is the smaller question, and the bigger one sits one layer
down: an assistant on top of a banking interface borrows the customer's authority and the customer can revoke it. An agent
inside the core acts with the institution's authority, so its actions are indistinguishable from the bank acting, and the person
who has to be able to stop it is not the customer.

The failure modes differ accordingly. On top, a bad action is an app misbehaving. Inside, it is the institution, and the record is
the one the institution keeps.

## Comment, paste as is

The trust question is the smaller one. The interesting difference is where the agent sits. An assistant on top of a banking interface borrows your authority, and you can revoke it. An agent inside the core acts with the bank's authority, so what it does is indistinguishable from the bank doing it, and the person who has to be able to stop it is no longer the customer.

That changes the failure mode. In the first case a bad action is an app misbehaving. In the second it is the institution, and the only record is the one the institution keeps about itself.

One prompt to book a flight is also the point where a bank starts carrying someone else's business, and someone else's liability with it.
