## Comment, paste as is

A listing is the first time this company has to write its claims where they can be checked every quarter, and the
interesting part of the filing is what has no field. The founders' letter promises cures. The risk factors warn
about models resisting shutdown. Both are prose, and neither is followed by a number for how the model behaves,
because the disclosure regime has fields for revenue, loss and commitments and none for measured capability or
measured failure.

Of all the figures, the 901 billion of commitments is the one that outlives the news cycle. It is a promise priced
at today's cost of compute and honoured at whatever that cost is in 2033, which is a different bet from the one the
valuation is making.

What I will be reading is the first 10-Q after ANT trades. If a model can be described as an existential risk in a
prospectus and then never described as a measured one in a quarterly report, we will have learned that the market
prices the language and never reads it.

## What not to say

His posts reach people who will price this thing, and almost every comment under them will be a restatement of a
number he already gave. A comment about the disclosure gap is the one thing in that thread that a reader cannot get
from the post itself, which is what makes it worth the slot rather than another agreement.
